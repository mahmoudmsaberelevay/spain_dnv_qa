Commission Tracking Database - Field Structure (from Notion browser inspection)

COLUMNS (in order):
1. Client Code - number/text (sequence number starting from 1)
2. Client Name - relation/text (linked to clients)
3. Status - select (Pending, Started, Cancelled)
4. Signing Date - date
5. Contract Value - number/currency (EUR)
6. Lead Source - select (Sales Mining, Referal, Marketing)
7. Qualifier Name - text/person (CS or CS TL from employees)
8. Payment Amount 1 (Qualifier Commission Payment) - number
9. cPayment Date (Qualifier Commission Date) - date
10. Qualifier Leader (Nouran) - text (CS TL name)
11. Amount 4 (Qualifier Leader Commission Amount) - number
12. Payment Date 1 (Qualifier Leader Commission Date) - date
13. (Monic)Operation TL Commission - number (Paralegal TL Commission Amount)
14. Date (Paralegal TL Commission Date) - date
15. (Madon)Operation Manager Commission - number
16. Date (Operation Manager Commission Date) - date
17. Paralegal - text/person (Paralegal or Paralegal TL or Operation Manager from employees)
18. First Payment Amount (Paralegal 1st Payment) - number
19. F. Payment Date (Paralegal 1st Payment Date) - date
20. Paralegal 2nd Payment - number
21. S. Payment Date (Paralegal 2nd Payment Date) - date
22. Paralegal 3rd Payment - number
23. 3rd Payment Date (Paralegal 3rd Payment Date) - date
24. CS (Consultant) - text/person (Consultant/Senior Consultant/Country Manager/CEO)
25. Payment Amounts For this Client - number (total)
26. First Payment 50% - number
27. Date of Payment (1st Payment Date) - date
28. Second Payment 25% - number
29. Date of Second (2nd Payment Date) - date
30. Third Payment 25% - number
31. Date of Third (3rd Payment Date) - date
32. Payment Date - date (Leader commission date)
33. Leader - text (Mahmoud Saber)
34. Amount 5 (Leader Commission Amount) - number
35. Total Expenses - formula/rollup
36. Expected Profit - formula/rollup
