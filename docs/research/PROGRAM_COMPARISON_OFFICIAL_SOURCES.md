# Program Comparison — Official Source Notes

Research date: 2026-09-09. These notes are implementation references; programme requirements and fees must be rechecked before client filing.

## Portugal National Residence Visas

Source: https://vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/national-visas/general-information/type-of-visa

Portugal's Ministry of Foreign Affairs states that residence visas allow two entries and remain valid for four months. During that period, the holder must apply to AIMA for a residence permit. The official national-visas page includes residence pathways for work, people living on their own income, independent activity/entrepreneurs, and professional activity performed remotely by digital nomads.

## Greece Golden Visa

Source: https://migration.gov.gr/en/golden-visa/

Greece's Ministry of Migration and Asylum identifies the route as an initial permanent residence permit for investors and publishes separate renewal requirements. The official page lists evidence of qualifying property/investment, health insurance, residence-card issuance fees, and continued ownership or lease conditions for renewal. Investment thresholds and route details must be verified against the current applicable legislation at filing.

## United Kingdom Expansion Worker

Source: https://www.gov.uk/uk-expansion-worker-visa

The official UK page states that this route is for a senior manager or specialist employee of an overseas business establishing a UK branch that has not begun trading. It requires sponsorship, qualifying overseas employment, an eligible occupation, and the applicable salary. Eligible dependants may accompany the worker. Initial permission is generally up to 12 months and may be extended, with a maximum of two years on this route. The route does not permit an application for permanent settlement.

## Canada Express Entry Skilled Migration

Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html

IRCC describes Express Entry as the online system managing the Canadian Experience Class, Federal Skilled Worker Program, and Federal Skilled Trades Program. Candidates create a profile, enter the pool, may receive an invitation based on points, and then apply for permanent residence. Processing varies by programme. The official page listed 2026 application fees of CAD 1,590 for the principal applicant, CAD 1,590 for a spouse, and CAD 270 per dependent child.

## Malta Permanent Residence Programme

Source: https://residencymalta.gov.mt/legal-framework-mprp-2/

Residency Malta describes MPRP as a residency-by-investment programme for non-EU, non-EEA, and non-Swiss nationals that grants permanent residence and is legally distinct from citizenship. The current official framework requires qualifying capital assets, licensed-agent submission, a €60,000 non-refundable administrative fee, a €37,000 government contribution, €7,500 for each adult dependant other than the spouse, qualifying property through at least €14,000 annual rent or a €375,000 purchase, a €2,000 NGO donation, health insurance, and residence-card fees. The qualifying property must be maintained for at least five years, followed by continued maintenance of residential property in Malta.

## Spain Digital Nomad Residency

Source: https://prie.comercio.gob.es/en-us/paginas/teletrabajadores-caracter-internacional.aspx

Spain's official international teleworker page states that non-EU foreigners may obtain residence while working remotely for companies outside Spain. Employees may work only for foreign companies; professionals may perform up to 20% of their activity for a Spanish company. The route requires suitable education or at least three years of professional experience, a real and continuing foreign company, a remotely performable relationship, and at least three months of prior employment or professional relationship. An overseas applicant may receive a visa for up to one year, while an eligible applicant legally in Spain may receive a three-year residence authorization.
