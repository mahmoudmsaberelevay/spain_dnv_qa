## Guardrails

- URLs are allowlist candidates, not legal advice or marketing facts.
- The knowledge library must save retrieval/published/effective dates, content hash/snapshot, review status, programme and issuing authority.
- Any eligibility, fee, policy or timeline statement must remain `proposed` until owner/compliance approval.
- No facts from a secondary source can replace a primary authority source.
- Material changes must create a new source version and a claim-review task; no existing approved content is silently altered.
