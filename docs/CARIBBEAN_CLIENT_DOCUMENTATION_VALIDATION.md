# Caribbean Client Documentation, Questionnaire, and Timeline — Validation Record

**Date:** 21 September 2026

## Scope

ELEVAY Client Documentation now supports **Spain, Grenada, Dominica, St. Kitts, St. Lucia, and Antigua**. Spain retains its established Digital Nomad Visa workflow. The five Caribbean programs use a separate questionnaire-first citizenship journey, an age- and relationship-aware checklist, a 13-step timeline, milestone payment rules, and protected Client Portal access.

## Client Documentation Creation

The New Client form defaults to Spain, preserving the existing Spain application subtype, Schengen, payment, consultant, and case-creation behavior. Selecting a Caribbean program hides Spain-only fields and explains that the journey begins with the secure questionnaire.

For Caribbean cases, staff enter marital status, dependant count, each dependant's relationship, optional name, and exact age. The form previews the document rule applied to each dependant before creation. The generated checklist is based on the supplied consolidated citizenship checklist and includes the 27-item main-applicant list, spouse items when applicable, and conditional dependant items.

The strict consolidated checklist applies the following conditions:

| Applicant situation | Generated requirement behavior |
|---|---|
| Child under 5 | Identity documents and photographs; medical items are not generated by default |
| Child age 5–15 | Identity, medical, immunisation, and education items |
| Child age 16–17 | Child items plus police clearances |
| Dependant age 18 or older | Adult identity, address, education/university, police, medical, and support-affidavit items |
| Dependant parent | Adult items plus marital-status, citizenship, and foreign-residence evidence |
| Spouse included | Dedicated spouse identity, marriage, address, education, police, medical, photograph, citizenship, and residence items |

Program-specific photograph quantities, police-clearance residence thresholds, medical requirements, and bank-statement periods remain represented by explicit “program-required” labels where the supplied master checklist states that the selected country's final specification controls.

## Client Questionnaire

A standalone public route, `/client-questionnaire`, loads without CRM authentication and presents a dedicated ELEVAY Client Portal sign-in. It does not expose CRM navigation or staff data. After secure Client Portal authentication, the client sees only assigned Caribbean applications.

The questionnaire is versioned and reconstructed from the supplied 17-page Word form. It presents **one question at a time**, supports Previous and Next navigation, continuously saves a bounded draft, resumes at the saved question, and validates the complete response before final submission. Repeatable table sections present **one row at a time**, with Previous Row, Next Row, Add Another Row, and Remove Row controls. Unknown fields are stripped, text and row counts are bounded, and a submitted questionnaire becomes immutable.

Submission stores the authoritative answers in the Client Documentation case, records an audited client lifecycle event, advances the case to Document Collection, and makes the organized response available to CRM staff by section. The client interface then displays the complete application journey.

## Caribbean Timeline and Staff Control

Consultants and paralegals can review the questionnaire and control the Caribbean stage and milestone dates from the Client Documentation case. The client interface and protected Client Portal workflow endpoints expose the same 13-step projection.

| Step | Stage or rule |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Sending the Questionnaire Form |
| 2 | Document Collection; starts after questionnaire submission |
| 3 | In the Legalization Process; starts automatically when all generated checklist documents are received |
| 4 | Submission Date; planned date set by consultant or paralegal |
| 5 | In Process; starts automatically as soon as the planned submission date is saved |
| 6 | Submission Payment; due 12 days before the planned submission date |
| 7 | Submitted; marked by consultant or paralegal with the actual submission date |
| 8 | Approved; marked with the approval date |
| 9 | Approval Payment; due from the day after approval |
| 10 | Issuing Naturalization Certificate |
| 11 | Naturalization Certificate Issued |
| 12 | Issuing of Passports |
| 13 | Passports Issued |

Chronology validation prevents naturalization or passport milestones from being dated out of order. Spain-only MOFA, Embassy, Schengen, travel, arrival, biometrics, and residency-card reminder logic is bypassed for Caribbean cases. Existing submission and approval payment reminders remain active with the Caribbean due-date rules.
