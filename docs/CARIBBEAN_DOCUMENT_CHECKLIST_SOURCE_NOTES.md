# Caribbean Citizenship Document Checklist — Source Notes

**Source:** `citizenship_application_document_checklist.pdf` supplied on 21 September 2026.

The attachment is a three-page consolidated master checklist. It explicitly warns that program-specific differences remain for photograph quantity and size, police-certificate age and residence thresholds, medical-test age, and bank-statement period. The first implementation therefore uses the strict consolidated union and preserves the program selected on the Client Documentation case.

## Master documents

The 27 source documents are: current passport (all pages), old passport identity/current-visa pages when applicable, detailed CV, birth certificate, national ID, main-applicant driving licence when held, marriage/divorce certificate when applicable, deceased-spouse death certificate for widowed applicants, education certificate for adults, university enrolment letter for current students, proof of address for the main applicant and adult dependants living elsewhere, professional reference, bank reference, employment letter, business licence/company documents, bank statements, recent passport photographs, police clearances, blood and urine tests, HIV test, immunisation schedule, sworn affidavit of support for adult dependants over 18 (excluding the spouse), affidavit of support for a sponsored applicant, signed letter to the Minister, military certificate when applicable, certificates of other citizenships, and foreign permanent-residence cards.

## Conditional family rules

| Situation | Conditional rule |
|---|---|
| Main applicant | Always receives core identity, financial, employment/business, reference, address, photograph, and compliance documents. |
| Spouse included | Passport, birth certificate, national ID, marriage certificate, photographs, adult education documents, medical tests, police certificates at the applicable age, and address evidence. |
| Child under 5 | Passport, birth certificate, photographs, and national ID if issued; listed medical requirements generally start at age 5. |
| Child age 5–15 | Identity documents and photographs plus blood/urine test, HIV testing under the strictest list, immunisation schedule, and education documents where applicable. |
| Child age 16–17 | All relevant child documents plus police certificates because one source list starts at age 16. |
| Child age 18 | Adult treatment: national ID, education certificate, separate proof of address if living elsewhere, police and medical documents, university enrolment if studying, and confirmation whether an affidavit is needed at exactly 18. |
| Dependant over 18 | Adult documents, university letter if studying, separate proof of address, police and medical documents, tax evidence where requested, and sworn affidavit of support. |
| Dependant parent | Passport, birth certificate, national ID, marital-status evidence, photographs, medical documents, police certificates, and citizenship/permanent-residence evidence where applicable. |
| Divorced or widowed | Divorce certificate or deceased spouse death certificate plus custody/name-change records when relevant. |
| External sponsor | Sponsor passport, CV where requested, affidavit of support, evidence of funds, and police certificates where applicable. |
| Residence abroad | Police certificates from every qualifying country of residence during the previous ten years. |
| Multiple citizenships / foreign residence | Citizenship or naturalisation certificates, foreign permanent-residence card/certificate, and related evidence. |

## Authentication and photograph notes

All photographs should be recent, identical, full frontal, neutral, eyes open, with no sunglasses, a plain white background, and compliant sizing for the selected program. Documents from Hague Convention countries generally require an apostille; other countries may require another legalisation route.
