## Program Overview

Portugal D7 Residence Visa

The D7 is a Portuguese residence visa for people who can show a regular pension or income from movable or immovable property, intellectual property or financial assets. After entry on the visa, a temporary residence permit is issued by the Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum (AIMA).

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Processing time | 3 to 6 months, based on Elevay’s experience. Not an official or guaranteed timeline. |
| Residence permit | 2 years; renewed in 3-year periods while the conditions are met. |
| Longest absence | 6 months in a row / 8 months in total, without justified reason, during the 2-year permit. |
| Means for one adult | EUR 11,040 a year, based on 12 x EUR 920 (2026 minimum wage). |

Based on the 2026 minimum wage of EUR 920 a month: EUR 920 x 12 = EUR 11,040 for one adult. Annual figures are calculated from the official formula; a spouse and children add to it. The income test follows a formula on the Portuguese minimum wage; there is no fixed D7 amount in the law. Government fees are listed at AIMA standard rates and can change.

Income to show for the D7

No fixed D7 amount is published. The means of subsistence are set by a formula on the 2026 minimum wage of EUR 920 a month, net of social security, and must be assured for at least 12 months.

What must be proved

| Visa stage | Residence permit stage (AIMA) |
|---|---|
| The general residency documents | Sworn declaration of the residential address, stating the basis of occupation |
| Proof of the retirement amount, or proof of income from movable or immovable property, intellectual property or financial assets | Owners: land-registry certificate. Tenants and hosts: a declaration from the landlord or host stating the legal basis |
| Criminal record certificate | Health insurance or national health service cover |
| Travel insurance | Tax and social security registration, where applicable |

Retirees prove the pension and that it is guaranteed to be received. Applicants with other income prove that the income exists, its amount, and that it is available in Portugal. This is not the full document checklist; see the separate Document Checklist.

Staying in Portugal and renewing

What the residence permit asks of the holder after approval. Please read the minimum-stay box in full.

The residence permit can be cancelled if you are absent from Portugal for more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 months in total, during its 2-year validity, without justified reason. In practice, plan to spend around 16 of the 24 months in Portugal.

| Period | Detail |
|---|---|
| 6 months in a row | The longest unjustified absence |
| 8 months in total | Unjustified absence within the 2 years |
| 16 of 24 months | A planning guide, not a legal minimum |

The law sets no minimum number of days. The 16-month figure is a planning guide calculated from the legal limits. Cancellation is possible, not automatic. Absences beyond these limits must be justified to the Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum (AIMA) before you leave. The law provides that the permit is not cancelled if the holder was carrying out professional, business, cultural or social activity while away. For a permanent residence permit, the limits are 24 consecutive months or 30 months in total within three years.

| Visa and permit item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Visa | Valid for two entries and 4 months. Legal decision deadline: 60 days (Law No. 23/2007, Art. 58). |
| Residence permit | Valid for 2 years, renewable in successive 3-year periods (Art. 75(1)). |
| Renewal conditions | Means of subsistence, accommodation, tax and social security obligations met, and no conviction above 1 year (Art. 78(2)). Renewal is never guaranteed. |

The permit can be cancelled after long unjustified absences. Renewal is never guaranteed.

Apostille or attestation: what is the difference?

| Route | What it means |
|---|---|
| Apostille | A single certificate added to a public document by the competent authority of a country that is a party to the Hague Apostille Convention (1961). Portugal is a party. For documents covered by the Convention, the apostille is the only formality that may be required to certify the signature, the capacity of the signatory and any seal or stamp. |
| Attestation | Also called legalisation, a longer route: the document is authenticated by the foreign ministry (MOFA) of the country that issued it and then by the Portuguese embassy or consulate. This is what the document checklist means by “attested by the Portuguese embassy and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs”. |

Which one applies depends on the country that issued the document. Translation into Portuguese is a separate step, as the checklist shows. Your Elevay consultant will confirm what applies to each of your documents.

## Passport access

Under Organic Law No. 1/2026, in force from 19 May 2026, Portuguese nationality by residence requires 10 years of legal residence, or 7 years for nationals of Portuguese-speaking countries and of European Union (EU) member states. Citizenship is never guaranteed or automatic, and other conditions apply. Applications filed before 19 May 2026 keep the earlier law.

Sources: Organic Law No. 1/2026 (justica.gov.pt, Nationality Law news item). Accessed 10 October 2026 unless stated.
