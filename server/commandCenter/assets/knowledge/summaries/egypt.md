PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Egypt Citizenship by Investment
Egyptian citizenship can be granted by the Prime Minister to a foreign investor under Law No. 140 of 2019. The
routes are a property purchase, an investment project, a cash deposit, or a direct revenue payment to the
government, with the spouse and children included in the application. Applications are made through the Egyptian
Citizenship by Investment unit, directly or through licensed intermediaries.

INSTALMENTS
The US$300,000 property route and the US$250,000 direct revenue route can be paid in instalments over no more
than one year. Citizenship is granted only after full payment, and the applicant receives a temporary non-tourist
residence during that time. If payment fails or the application is withdrawn, amounts paid are refunded in Egyptian
pounds at the Central Bank rate on the refund date, capped at the rate on the payment date, without interest.

HOW THE APPLICATION WORKS
No. Step
1 Submit the application and documents, in person at the unit's office in Cairo or through licensed immigration
intermediaries.
2 Pay the non-refundable USS10,000 application fee.
3 The unit confirms receipt within three working days, then reviews the file and issues a preliminary decision.
4 With the Prime Minister's preliminary approval, the applicant has the option of a six-month temporary residence
while completing the chosen investment route. Residence is not arequirement.
5 The Prime Minister issues the final decision, and citizenship is granted through a certificate of naturalisation.
LEGAL BASIS
Law No. 140 of 2019 allows the Prime Minister to grant Egyptian citizenship to foreigners. The grants are regulated
by Prime Minister Decisions No. 3099 of 2019 and No. 3562 of 2023, and the Cabinet unit reviewing applications
was formed by Decision No. 647 of 2020. Citizenship can be revoked within ten years if obtained through fraud or
false statements.
