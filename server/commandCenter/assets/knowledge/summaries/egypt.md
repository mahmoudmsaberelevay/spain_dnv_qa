## 1. Program Overview

Egypt Citizenship by Investment

Egyptian citizenship can be granted by the Prime Minister to a foreign investor under Law No. 140 of 2019. The routes are a property purchase, an investment project, a cash deposit, or a direct revenue payment to the government, with the spouse and children included in the application. Applications are made through the Egyptian Citizenship by Investment unit, directly or through licensed intermediaries.

- 9–12 months* — Typical time to citizenship, based on current Elevay files.
- None — Residence requirement.
- 5 years — Holding period for the real estate and investment project routes.
- 61† — Visa-free, visa-on-arrival and eTA destinations.

* Based on Elevay’s experience processing current files. The official site states 6 to 12 months. This is not a guaranteed timeline and varies by case. † Passport Index, accessed 8 October 2026; indicative only (see the passport access page).

### How the application works

1. Submit the application and documents, in person at the unit’s office in Cairo or through licensed immigration intermediaries.
2. Pay the non-refundable US$10,000 application fee.
3. The unit confirms receipt within three working days, then reviews the file and issues a preliminary decision.
4. With the Prime Minister’s preliminary approval, the applicant has the option of a six-month temporary residence while completing the chosen investment route. Residence is not a requirement.
5. The Prime Minister issues the final decision, and citizenship is granted through a certificate of naturalisation.

### Legal basis

Law No. 140 of 2019 allows the Prime Minister to grant Egyptian citizenship to foreigners. The grants are regulated by Prime Minister Decisions No. 3099 of 2019 and No. 3562 of 2023, and the Cabinet unit reviewing applications was formed by Decision No. 647 of 2020. Citizenship can be revoked within ten years if obtained through fraud or false statements.

## 2. Who Can be Included

- The spouse of the main applicant. The spouse is included in the fees with no further fees and can apply after two years of marriage, counted from the issue of the main applicant’s Egyptian citizenship.
- Children under 21.
- All nationalities may apply. National security agencies are consulted on every file.
- There is no language test, so Arabic is not required.
- Egyptian law allows Egyptian citizens to hold other nationalities.
- There is no residency requirement. A six-month temporary residence is available as an option within the application.

## 3. Investment routes

| Route | Amount | Holding period |
|---|---|---|
| Property purchase | US$300,000, transferred from abroad | 5 years |
| Investment project | US$350,000 invested in the project, plus a US$100,000 donation to the government | 5 years |
| Cash deposit | US$500,000, at the Central Bank of Egypt | 3 years |
| Direct revenue (government) | US$250,000, non-refundable | N/A |

Two routes require no property or business. One is a refundable deposit held at the Central Bank of Egypt, the other is a non-refundable direct revenue payment to the government.

Both routes require funds to be transferred from abroad, and the investment project must meet Investment Law No. 72 of 2017.

**Property purchase**

Purchase of a property for no less than US$300,000, transferred from abroad. If the property is sold within five years, retaining citizenship requires a non-refundable payment of US$250,000 to the state treasury through the Central Bank of Egypt.

**Investment project**

An investment project with capital of no less than US$350,000 transferred from abroad and invested in the project, plus a US$100,000 donation to the government. If the project is liquidated or the holding is disposed of within five years of operation, a further non-refundable US$250,000 is due.

**Cash deposit**

US$500,000, deposited by foreign bank transfer into a special account at the Central Bank of Egypt for three years. The deposit is returned in Egyptian pounds at the Central Bank rate on the refund date, without interest.

**Direct revenue**

US$250,000, paid by foreign bank transfer as direct revenue to the government. The payment is non-refundable and can be made in instalments over no more than one year.

**Instalments**

The US$300,000 property route and the US$250,000 direct revenue route can be paid in instalments over no more than one year. Citizenship is granted only after full payment, and the applicant receives a temporary non-tourist residence during that time. If payment fails or the application is withdrawn, amounts paid are refunded in Egyptian pounds at the Central Bank rate on the refund date, capped at the rate on the payment date, without interest.

## 4. Contribution options

- Investment project: US$350,000 invested in the project, plus a US$100,000 donation to the government.
- Direct revenue (government): US$250,000, non-refundable.

## 8. Passport access

Travel with an Egyptian passport

The number of destinations that an Egyptian passport holder can currently enter without a prior visa application, with a visa on arrival, or with an electronic travel authorisation (eTA).

| Category | Number |
|---|---:|
| Visa-free destinations | 19 |
| Visa-on-arrival destinations | 37 |
| eTA destinations | 5 |
| Total including eTA | 61 |

Source: Passport Index (passportindex.org), accessed 8 October 2026. The total is the sum of the three categories shown.

### What the categories mean

- Visa-free: no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- Visa on arrival: the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- eTA: an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
