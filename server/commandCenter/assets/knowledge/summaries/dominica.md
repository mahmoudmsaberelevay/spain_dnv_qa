## 1. Program Overview

Dominica Citizenship by Investment

Citizenship of Dominica can be obtained through a contribution to the Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) or through an investment in an approved real estate project, with qualifying family members included in a single application. The programme is administered by the Citizenship by Investment Unit (CBIU), and applications are submitted only through authorised agents.

- **9–12 months\*:** Full process, based on current Elevay files
- **None‡:** No requirement to reside in Dominica
- **3 or 5 years:** Holding period for the real estate route
- **135†:** Visa-free, visa-on-arrival and eTA destinations

\* Based on Elevay’s experience processing current files. This is not an official or guaranteed timeline and varies by case.

† Passport Index, accessed 8 October 2026; indicative only (see the passport access page).

‡ CBIU FAQs: applicants do not have to reside in Dominica before or after citizenship is granted.

## 2. Who Can be Included

- The spouse of the main applicant
- A child under 18 of the main applicant or spouse
- A child aged 18 to 30 of the main applicant or spouse, in higher education and fully supported (a child who has turned 31 cannot be included)
- An unmarried daughter under 25, living with and fully supported by the applicant
- A child aged 18 or over who is physically or mentally challenged and fully supported
- Parents or grandparents over 65 who are substantially supported

### GOOD TO KNOW

- The main applicant must be at least 18 years old.
- A mandatory interview applies to applicants aged 16 and over, usually conducted virtually.
- Some nationalities are banned, suspended or restricted: see page 4.
- Dominica allows dual citizenship, with no renunciation required.

## 3. Investment routes

### INVESTMENT ROUTES AT A GLANCE

| Route | Minimum investment | Holding period |
|---|---|---|
| Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) | US$200,000 (single) or US$250,000 (main applicant plus up to three dependants) | N/A |
| Approved real estate | US$200,000, plus government fees from US$75,000 | 3 or 5 years |

### OPTION 2

### Real Estate Investment

Citizenship can also be obtained by purchasing a unit in a government-approved real estate project, such as a resort or residence. Government fees are payable if the application is approved, in addition to the investment.

### HOLDING PERIOD

3 or 5 years

After three years, the property can be sold to a buyer who is not applying for citizenship. After five years, it can be sold to a buyer who will apply for citizenship through it.

### BANNED, SUSPENDED AND RESTRICTED NATIONALITIES

Banned: Belarus, Russia, and Northern Iraq (nationals of Erbil, Amedi, Dahuk, Kirkuk, Lalish, Raniya, Soran, Sulaymaniyah and Zakho). Suspended: Yemen.

Restricted: applications from nationals of Iran, North Korea and Sudan are refused unless the applicant (a) has not lived in the country for at least ten years, (b) holds no substantial assets there, and (c) has conducted no business or similar activity in or with the country.

Enhanced due diligence is conducted on all Iranian applicants and may apply to other applicants who meet the conditions above, at the applicant’s cost.

## 4. Contribution options

### OPTION 1

### Economic Diversification Fund

The Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) is a government fund that supports Dominica’s economic growth and diversification. The contribution is non-refundable and is made after approval in principle, through an authorised agent. No government fees apply to the EDF route.

### OPTION 1

### Single applicant

US$200,000 for the main applicant.

### OPTION 1

### Family

US$250,000 for the main applicant and up to three qualifying dependants.

### WHAT THE CATEGORIES MEAN

- Visa-free: no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- Visa on arrival: the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- eTA: an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
