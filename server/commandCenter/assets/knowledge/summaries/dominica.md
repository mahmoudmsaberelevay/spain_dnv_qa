PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Dominica Citizenship by Investment
Citizenship of Dominica can be obtained through a contribution to the Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) or
through an investment in an approved real estate project, with qualifying family members included ina single
application. The programme is administered by the Citizenship by Investment Unit (CBIU), and applications are
submitted only through authorised agents.

OPTION 1
Economic Diversification Fund
The Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) is a government fund that supports Dominica's economic growth and
diversification. The contribution is non-refundable and is made after approval in principle, through an authorised
agent. No government fees apply to the EDF route.

OPTION 2
Real Estate Investment
Citizenship can also be obtained by purchasing a unit in a government-approved real estate project, such as a
resort or residence. Government fees are payable if the application is approved, in addition to the investment.

BANNED, SUSPENDED AND RESTRICTED NATIONALITIES
Banned: Belarus, Russia, and Northern Iraq (nationals of Erbil, Amedi, Dahuk, Kirkuk, Lalish, Raniya, Soran,
Sulaymaniyah and Zakho). Suspended: Yemen.
Restricted: applications from nationals of Iran, North Korea and Sudan are refused unless the applicant (a) has not
lived in the country for at least ten years, (b) holds no substantial assets there, and (c) has conducted no business or
similar activity in or with the country.
Enhanced due diligence is conducted on all Iranian applicants and may apply to other applicants who meet the
conditions above, at the applicant's cost.
