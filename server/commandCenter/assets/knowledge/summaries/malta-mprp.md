## Program Overview

Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP)

The Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP) is a Maltese residence programme for non-European Union (EU), non-European Economic Area (EEA) and non-Swiss nationals. The applicant either rents or buys a qualifying home in Malta or Gozo, makes the required government payments and shows the required capital. It gives the right to stay and reside permanently in Malta and visa-free travel across the Schengen Area for 90 days in any 180-day period. It does not give citizenship by itself. Applications are made to Residency Malta, the government agency that runs the programme (the Agency).

Processing takes about 6 to 9 months based on Elevay’s experience. This is not an official or guaranteed timeline; the Agency publishes no processing time. The Agency says decisions are communicated within a reasonable timeframe and that processing starts when a complete and correct file is submitted.

A temporary residence permit valid for 1 year can be applied for when the application is submitted, for the applicant and dependants.

The MPRP regulations set no minimum number of days a year in Malta. Hold a qualifying home as your residence for at least 5 years. After that, hold a residential property in Malta or Gozo, owned or leased. Keep the capital assets for 5 years, and keep the required health insurance. Your permanent residence can be withdrawn if you stop meeting the conditions (for example, you no longer hold the property or keep the capital), if false information was given in the application, or if the authorities decide that your stay is against the public interest.

The MPRP does not give citizenship by itself, and buying or renting a property under it gives no right to citizenship. A beneficiary who actually lives in Malta may apply for Maltese citizenship by naturalisation under the general citizenship law, like any other foreign resident. The law asks for residence in Malta throughout the 12 months before applying and for at least 4 years in the 6 years before that, adequate knowledge of Maltese or English, good character and suitability. The Minister decides each case, so approval is never guaranteed. If the beneficiary becomes a Maltese national, the MPRP certificate ceases.

Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved.

Stable and regular resources enough to support the applicant and dependants without Malta’s social assistance.

Every application goes through multi-tier due diligence. Approval is never guaranteed.

## Investment routes

The main applicant meets the property requirement in one of two ways, rent or buy, and keeps the capital for 5 years.

### Option A: Rent

EUR 14,000 a year

Rent a residential property in Malta or Gozo for at least EUR 14,000 a year, and hold the lease as your residence for at least 5 years.

### Option B: Buy

EUR 375,000

Buy a residential property in Malta or Gozo worth at least EUR 375,000, and hold it as your residence for at least 5 years.

Either way, the property must be held as the residence for a minimum of 5 years. After 5 years, the beneficiary must still hold a residential property in Malta or Gozo, owned or leased. With the Agency’s consent the property can be replaced with another qualifying one. A property bought for less than EUR 375,000 counts if works paid by the main applicant bring its value to at least EUR 375,000. The Agency may carry out spot checks of the property.

### Capital assets

| Option | Capital assets | Of which financial assets |
|---|---:|---:|
| Option 1 | at least EUR 500,000 | EUR 150,000 |
| Option 2 | at least EUR 650,000 | EUR 75,000 |

The capital must be kept for 5 years. The Agency decides in its sole discretion which assets count as financial assets.

## Government Processing fees

### Government fees and payment timing

| Fee | Amount | Who pays and when |
|---|---:|---|
| Administration fee, non-refundable | EUR 60,000 | Main applicant, in two instalments: EUR 15,000 within 1 month of submitting the application and EUR 45,000 within 2 months of the Letter of Approval in Principle. |
| Government contribution | EUR 37,000 | Main applicant, within 8 months of the Approval in Principle. The same amount for owned or rented property. |
| Dependant administration fee | EUR 7,500 each | For children aged 18 to 28, and for parents and grandparents. No fee for the spouse or partner, children under 18, or adult children with a certified disability. |
| Donation | EUR 2,000 | Main applicant, to a registered local non-governmental organisation (NGO). |
| Residence card | EUR 500 per person | Each person; covers 5 years. Agency brochure figure; not printed in the law. |

Also required: health insurance covering all risks normally covered for Maltese nationals. Government fees can vary. Property purchase or rental costs and professional fees are separate and are not included here.

### Payment timeline

| When | Payment |
|---|---|
| Within 1 month of submitting the application | EUR 15,000 first instalment of the administration fee |
| Within 2 months of the Letter of Approval in Principle | EUR 45,000 second instalment of the administration fee |
| Within 8 months of the Approval in Principle | EUR 37,000 government contribution |

No total is shown. The Agency does not publish a total, and the final cost depends on the number of dependants and on the property. Your Elevay consultant will prepare a personal cost estimate.
