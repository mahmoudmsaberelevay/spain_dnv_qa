PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP)
The Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP) is a Maltese residence programme for non-European Union
(EU), non-European Economic Area (EEA) and non-Swiss nationals. The applicant either rents or buys a qualifying
home in Malta or Gozo, makes the required government payments and shows the required capital. It gives the right
to stay and reside permanently in Malta and visa-free travel across the Schengen Area for 90 days in any 180-day
period. It does not give citizenship by itself (see page 5). Applications are made to Residency Malta, the
government agency that runs the programme (the Agency).

PERMIT AND BENEFITS
What the programme gives, and what to keep
Item Detail
Right to reside The MPRP gives the right to stay and reside permanently in Malta.
Schengen travel Visa-free travel across the Schengen Area for 90 days in any 180-day period.
Temporary permit A permit valid for 1 year can be applied for when the application is submitted, for the
applicant and dependants.
Work and business The MPRP gives you the right to live in Malta. It does not automatically give you the right
to work. If you plan to work or start a business in Malta, you must also meet Malta's
normal rules for that. Your Elevay consultant will explain what applies.
KEEPING THE STATUS
e¢ The MPRP regulations set no minimum number of days a year in Malta.
e Hold a qualifying home as your residence for at least 5 years. After that, hold a residential property in Malta or Gozo, owned or
leased.
e Keep the capital assets for 5 years, and keep the required health insurance.
e Your permanent residence can be withdrawn if you stop meeting the conditions (for example, you no longer hold the property
or keep the capital), if false information was given in the application, or if the authorities decide that your stay is against the
public interest.
CITIZENSHIP
The MPRP does not give citizenship by itself, and buying or renting a property under it gives no right to citizenship.
A beneficiary who actually lives in Malta may apply for Maltese citizenship by naturalisation under the general citizenship
law, like any other foreign resident. The law asks for residence in Malta throughout the 12 months before applying and for
at least 4 years in the 6 years before that, adequate knowledge of Maltese or English, good character and suitability. The
Minister decides each case, so approval is never guaranteed. If the beneficiary becomes a Maltese national, the MPRP
certificate ceases.
TIMING
Based on Elevay’s experience, processing takes about 6 to 9 months. This is not an official or guaranteed timeline. The Agency
publishes no processing time. It says decisions are communicated within a reasonable timeframe and that processing starts when
a complete and correct file is submitted.
