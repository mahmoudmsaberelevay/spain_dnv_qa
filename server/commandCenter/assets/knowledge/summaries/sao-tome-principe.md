## 1. Program Overview

São Tomé and Príncipe Citizenship by Investment

São Tomé and Príncipe grants citizenship in return for a contribution to the National Transformation Fund, under Nationality Law No. 07/2022 and Decree-Law No. 07/2025. Citizenship acquired through the programme carries no distinction from citizenship by birth.

*Based on Elevay’s experience processing current files. This is not an official or guaranteed timeline and varies by case. †Passport Index, accessed 8 October 2026; indicative only (see the passport access page).

There is no residency requirement. There is no requirement to give up your existing nationality, and the citizenship can be passed to all future generations. Citizenship acquired through the programme carries no distinction from citizenship by birth. No government fees apply beyond those listed in this summary. All amounts are in US dollars.

Sources: cip.gov.st (programme and Qualifying Dependents pages, as supplied by Elevay), accessed 4 and 9 October 2026.

## 2. Who Can be Included

- Spouse: monogamous marriage or de facto partnership
- Children: unmarried, up to 30 years old
- Parents and grandparents: aged 55 and above

Dependants can be included in the application or added after approval in principle (see the inclusion fees). The main applicant must be at least 18 years old.

Sources: cip.gov.st (programme and Qualifying Dependents pages, as supplied by Elevay), accessed 4 and 9 October 2026.

## 3. Investment routes

One route is offered: a contribution to the National Transformation Fund, plus government fees for the application and the citizenship documents.

### National Transformation Fund

The minimum contribution is due on approval in principle. It depends on the number of people in the application: US$90,000 for a single applicant and US$95,000 for a family of 2 to 4, plus US$5,000 for each additional dependant.

The programme rests on Nationality Law No. 07/2022 and Decree-Law No. 07/2025. The programme site states that citizenship acquired through the programme carries no distinction from citizenship by birth.

Sources: cip.gov.st (programme and Benefits pages, as supplied by Elevay), accessed 4 October 2026.

Source: Passport Index (passportindex.org), accessed 8 October 2026. The total is the sum of the three categories shown.

### What the categories mean

- Visa-free: no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- Visa on arrival: the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- eTA: an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
