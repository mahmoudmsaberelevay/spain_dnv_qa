PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Sao Tomé and Principe Citizenship by Investment
Sao Tomé and Principe grants citizenship in return for a contribution to the National Transformation Fund, under
Nationality Law No. 07/2022 and Decree-Law No. 07/2025. Citizenship acquired through the programme carries
no distinction from citizenship by birth.

THE PROGRAMME
Contribution and fees
One route is offered: a contribution to the National Transformation Fund, plus government fees for the application
and the citizenship documents.
NATIONAL TRANSFORMATION FUND
Contribution
The minimum contribution is due on approval in principle. It depends on the number of people in the application:
US$90,000 for a single applicant and USS95,000 for a family of 2 to 4, plus USS5,000 for each additional
dependant.

FAMILY AND LEGAL BASIS
Adding family members and legal framework

LEGAL BASIS
The programme rests on Nationality Law No. 07/2022 and Decree-Law No. 07/2025. The programme site states
that citizenship acquired through the programme carries no distinction from citizenship by birth.
TIMING
The Elevay time to citizenship of 60 to 90 days is based on current files and is not an official or guaranteed timeline.
It varies by case.
