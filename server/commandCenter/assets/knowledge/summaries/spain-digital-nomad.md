PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Spain Digital Nomad Visa
Spain's Digital Nomad Visa (the law calls it the visa and residence authorisation for international teleworkers) is
for adult nationals of countries outside the European Union (EU) who work remotely, using only information
technology and telecommunication means, for companies located outside Spain. The programme is open: Articles
74 bis to 74 quinquies of Law 14/2013 are in force.

ELIGIBILITY AND INCOME
Who can apply and the income to show
Who Adult third-country nationals only. Citizens of the EU and people covered by EU law are
excluded. Holders of anon-lucrative residence permit cannot apply on that permit,
because it does not allow work.
Way of working Remote work for companies located outside Spain, using exclusively information
technology and telecommunication means. An employee may work only for companies
outside Spain. A professional (self-employed) may also serve a Spanish company for up
to 20% of their total professional activity (Article 74 bis).
Qualifications A degree or postgraduate qualification from a recognised university, vocational school
or business school, or at least 3 years of professional experience (Article 74 bis).
The foreign company The company or group must show real, continuous activity for at least 1 year (Article 74
ter).
The working relationship The employment or professional relationship with the company outside Spain must have
lasted at least the last 3 months before applying, with documents showing that remote
work is allowed or the terms of the remote activity. This proof is not asked of someone
who already holds a teleworker visa or an earlier initial authorisation.
General conditions Over 18, no criminal record, health insurance, sufficient resources and the fee paid
(Article 62.3).
INCOME TO SHOW
Main applicant 200% of the minimum wage. The annual 2026 minimum wage is EUR 17,094 (Royal
Decree 126/2026, Article 3, in force from 20 February 2026), so 200% is EUR 34,188 a
year (gross).
Family unit of two The holder plus one family member: at least 75% of the annual minimum wage for the
family member: EUR 12,820.50 a year (gross).
Each further member 25% of the annual minimum wage for each further family member: EUR 4,273.50 a year
each (gross).
Amounts are gross, before tax and Social Security. The UGE states the requirement as a percentage of the minimum wage and does not print a
euro total; the annual figures are Elevay’s calculation from the 2026 minimum wage in Royal Decree 126/2026. “UGE” is the Large Companies
and Strategic Groups Unit of the Spanish Inclusion Ministry (Unidad de Grandes Empresas y Colectivos Estratégicos).

Two ways to apply, and how long each stage can take
The two routes are separate paths. Each has its own validity. The periods are not added together, because the law gives
each one separately and does not state how they combine.

FAMILY, SOCIAL SECURITY AND TAX
Family members, RETA registration and tax
FAMILY MEMBERS
Family member What the law and the UGE say
Spouse or partner Spouse or person in an analogous affective relationship. An unmarried, unregistered
partner shows at least 1 year of continuous cohabitation, unless there are common children.
Children Minor children, and adult children who depend economically on the holder and have not
formed their own family unit. Aged 18 to under 26: no income from work or public benefits,
and enrolled in a qualifying course or registered as a job-seeker. Aged 26 or over: only with
an incapacity that prevents them from being economically independent.
Parents and Dependent ascendants show economic or physical dependence; over 80 is presumed. Family
grandparents members may apply with the holder or later, and may work in Spain.
REGISTER WITH RETA STRAIGHT AWAY
Self-employed holders must register with the self-employed Social Security scheme (RETA) as soon as the authorisation
is granted and before starting work in Spain. Late registration can end the authorisation, and the holder and dependants
can then be barred from living and working in Spain. Do not wait. Employees register with the General Regime, or show a
certificate that home-country cover applies.
TARIFA PLANA AND RETA CONTRIBUTIONS
Tarifa plana is a reduced monthly quota for new self-employed workers: from about EUR 88.56 a month for the first 12 months.
A further 12 months is possible only if net annual income is below the annual minimum wage. It must be requested when you
register, and is not available if you were registered in RETA in the previous 2 years. After the reduced period you pay an
income-based quota, which depends on your net income.
CHECK THE CURRENT RETA CONTRIBUTIONS
The Spanish Social Security administration publishes the current contribution tables and a simulator. Please check them
here: seg-social.es, “Nuevo sistema de cotizaci6n para autonomos”. The quota is the Social Security cost only; it excludes
tax and any adviser costs.
TAX: SEEK YOUR OWN ADVICE
Elevay does not give tax advice. Tax residence and personal tax treatment are not set by the immigration authority. It is
very important that you seek your own independent tax advice before you apply.
