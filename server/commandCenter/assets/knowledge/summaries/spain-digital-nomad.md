## Program Overview

Spain Digital Nomad Visa

Spain’s Digital Nomad Visa (the law calls it the visa and residence authorisation for international teleworkers) is for adult nationals of countries outside the European Union (EU) who work remotely, using only information technology and telecommunication means, for companies located outside Spain. The programme is open: Articles 74 bis to 74 quinquies of Law 14/2013 are in force.

Up to 20 days: Legal maximum for the residence authorisation, from electronic filing. This is a legal maximum, not a promise. Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved.

Route 1: Up to 1 year. Teleworking visa issued by a Spanish consulate.

Route 2: Up to 3 years. Residence authorisation, renewable in 2-year periods while conditions are met.

The two routes are separate paths. Each has its own validity. The periods are not added together, because the law gives each one separately and does not state how they combine.

ROUTE 1 — From abroad, through a Spanish consulate

For non-residents who plan to live in Spain and telework for a company outside Spain. They apply for the international teleworking visa. Visas are issued by Spanish diplomatic missions and consulates. The visa is valid for a maximum of 1 year, or for the same period as the work if that is shorter. While valid, it is sufficient title to live and work remotely in Spain. Within the 60 calendar days before the visa expires, the holder may apply for the residence authorisation if the conditions still hold.

ROUTE 2 — From inside Spain

For people already lawfully in Spain, or who entered on the teleworker visa. They apply for the residence authorisation with the UGE. The authorisation is valid for the time requested, up to a maximum of 3 years. It can be renewed in 2-year periods while the conditions that gave the right still hold. If the authorisation lasts more than 6 months, the holder must then apply for the foreigner identity card.

PROCESSING TIME (LEGAL MAXIMUM)

The UGE handles the residence authorisation electronically. The law sets a maximum of 20 days from electronic filing. If no decision is made in that time, the authorisation is deemed granted by administrative silence (Article 76.1).

This is the legal maximum, not a promise. It does not include the time needed to prepare the documents. Renewal is never guaranteed.

Two application routes exist: from abroad through a Spanish consulate, or from inside Spain. Income is shown as one gross annual figure. Register with the self-employed scheme (RETA) straight away after the authorisation. Renewal is never promised: it depends on the conditions still being met.

## Who Can be Included

Spouse or partner in an analogous relationship.

An unmarried, unregistered partner shows at least 1 year of continuous cohabitation, unless there are common children.

Minor children.

Adult children who depend economically on the holder and have not formed their own family unit: aged 18 to under 26 if they meet the UGE conditions, and 26 or over only with an incapacity.

Aged 18 to under 26: no income from work or public benefits, and enrolled in a qualifying course or registered as a job-seeker. Aged 26 or over: only with an incapacity that prevents them from being economically independent.

Dependent parents and grandparents. Dependent ascendants show economic or physical dependence; over 80 is presumed.

Family members may apply with the holder or later, and may work in Spain without restriction, as employees or self-employed.

Who can apply

Adult third-country nationals only. Citizens of the EU and people covered by EU law are excluded. Holders of a non-lucrative residence permit cannot apply on that permit, because it does not allow work.

Way of working

Remote work for companies located outside Spain, using exclusively information technology and telecommunication means. An employee may work only for companies outside Spain. A professional (self-employed) may also serve a Spanish company for up to 20% of their total professional activity (Article 74 bis).

Qualifications

A degree or postgraduate qualification from a recognised university, vocational school or business school, or at least 3 years of professional experience (Article 74 bis).

The foreign company

The company or group must show real, continuous activity for at least 1 year (Article 74 ter).

The working relationship

The employment or professional relationship with the company outside Spain must have lasted at least the last 3 months before applying, with documents showing that remote work is allowed or the terms of the remote activity. This proof is not asked of someone who already holds a teleworker visa or an earlier initial authorisation.

General conditions

Over 18, no criminal record, health insurance, sufficient resources and the fee paid (Article 62.3).

## Contribution options

REGISTER WITH RETA STRAIGHT AWAY

Self-employed holders must register with the self-employed Social Security scheme (RETA) as soon as the authorisation is granted and before starting work in Spain. Late registration can end the authorisation, and the holder and dependants can then be barred from living and working in Spain. Do not wait. Employees register with the General Regime, or show a certificate that home-country cover applies.

TARIFA PLANA AND RETA CONTRIBUTIONS

Tarifa plana is a reduced monthly quota for new self-employed workers: from about EUR 88.56 a month for the first 12 months. A further 12 months is possible only if net annual income is below the annual minimum wage. It must be requested when you register, and is not available if you were registered in RETA in the previous 2 years. After the reduced period you pay an income-based quota, which depends on your net income.

CHECK THE CURRENT RETA CONTRIBUTIONS

The Spanish Social Security administration publishes the current contribution tables and a simulator. Please check them here: seg-social.es, “Nuevo sistema de cotización para autónomos”. The quota is the Social Security cost only; it excludes tax and any adviser costs.

Contribution information: Social Security administration, not the UGE. Tarifa plana of about EUR 88.56: Elevay figure, to be confirmed on an official page.
