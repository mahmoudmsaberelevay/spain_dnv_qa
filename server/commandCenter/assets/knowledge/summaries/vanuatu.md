## 1. Program Overview

Vanuatu Citizenship by Investment

Vanuatu grants honorary citizenship under its Development Support Program (DSP) in return for a non-refundable contribution to the programme. Applications are reviewed and approved by the Vanuatu Citizenship Commission, and the main applicant can include their spouse, dependent children and dependent parents.

Citizenship is granted under the Citizenship Act [Cap 112]. Honorary citizenship is provided for in Section 20 of the Act, and the Citizenship (Development Support Program) Regulations are published as Order No. 33 of 2019.

## 2. Who Can be Included

- The spouse of the main applicant
- Dependent children up to the age of 25
- Dependent parents of the main applicant or the spouse, aged 50 or older

## 3. Investment routes

One route is offered: a non-refundable contribution to the Development Support Program, scaled by the number of people in the application.

### Development Support Program

The applicant makes a non-refundable contribution to the Development Support Program. Subject to screening and approval by the Vanuatu Citizenship Commission, the applicant and the family members included in the application are granted honorary citizenship under the programme. The amount depends on the size of the family.

Source: Passport Index (passportindex.org), accessed 8 October 2026. The total is the sum of the three categories shown.

### What the categories mean

- Visa-free: no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- Visa on arrival: the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- eTA: an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
