PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Vanuatu Citizenship by Investment
Vanuatu grants honorary citizenship under its Development Support Program (DSP) in return for a non-refundable
contribution to the programme. Applications are reviewed and approved by the Vanuatu Citizenship Commission,
and the main applicant can include their spouse, dependent children and dependent parents.

THE PROGRAMME
Development Support Program
One route is offered: a non-refundable contribution to the Development Support Program, scaled by the number
of people in the application.
DEVELOPMENT SUPPORT PROGRAM
Non-refundable contribution
The applicant makes a non-refundable contribution to the Development Support Program. Subject to screening and
approval by the Vanuatu Citizenship Commission, the applicant and the family members included in the application
are granted honorary citizenship under the programme. The amount depends on the size of the family.

LEGAL BASIS
Citizenship is granted under the Citizenship Act [Cap 112]. Honorary citizenship is provided for in Section 20 of the
Act, and the Citizenship (Development Support Program) Regulations are published as Order No. 33 of 2019.
