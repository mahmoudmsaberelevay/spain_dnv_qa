## 1. Program Overview

Saint Lucia Citizenship by Investment

Citizenship of Saint Lucia can be obtained through a contribution to the National Economic Fund (NEF), the purchase of a National Action Bond (NAB), or an investment in an approved real estate or enterprise project, with qualifying family members included in a single application. The programme is administered by the Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU), and applications are submitted through authorised agents.

Applications go through an authorised agent. Saint Lucia allows dual citizenship, with no renunciation required.

## 2. Who Can be Included

- The spouse of the main applicant
- Children aged 21 or under, and children up to 30 who are fully supported by the main applicant
- Children of any age who are physically or mentally challenged and fully supported
- Parents aged over 55 who are fully supported by the main applicant, and parents of any age who are physically or mentally challenged and fully supported
- Unmarried siblings under 18, with parental consent

Due diligence is conducted only on applicants above the age of 16. An applicant interview and identity verification process applies to all applications. It is payable by the main applicant only.

## 3. Investment routes

| Route | Minimum investment | Holding period |
|---|---|---|
| National Economic Fund (NEF) | US$240,000 (applicant with up to three dependants) | N/A |
| National Action Bond (NAB) | US$300,000, plus a US$50,000 fee | 5 years |
| Approved real estate | US$300,000, plus administration fees | 5 years |
| Approved enterprise project | From US$250,000 (with dependants), US$3,500,000 (sole developer) or US$6,000,000 (joint venture) | 5 years |

### National Economic Fund and National Action Bond

The National Economic Fund (NEF) is a government fund that finances projects benefiting Saint Lucia’s economy. The National Action Bond (NAB) is a bond issued by the Government of Saint Lucia. Both routes are made through an authorised agent.

#### Option 1 — National Economic Fund

US$240,000 for the applicant alone or with up to three dependants.

#### Option 2 — National Action Bond

US$300,000 for the applicant with any number of dependants, plus a non-refundable fee of US$50,000.

Five-year holding period from the date of first issue.

### Approved real estate and enterprise projects

Citizenship can also be obtained by investing in a project approved by the Citizenship by Investment Unit. Projects must demonstrate clearly viable commercial, operational and financial proposals.

#### Option 3 — Approved real estate

A minimum investment of US$300,000 in an approved high-end branded hotel or resort (70+ rooms) or boutique property (20 to 70 rooms), plus administration fees.

#### Option 4 — Approved enterprise project

Sole developer: US$3,500,000 minimum, at least 3 permanent jobs, plus a US$50,000 fee. Joint venture: US$6,000,000 combined, at least US$1,000,000 per investor, at least 6 permanent jobs, plus a US$50,000 fee.

### What the categories mean

- Visa-free: no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- Visa on arrival: the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- eTA: an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
