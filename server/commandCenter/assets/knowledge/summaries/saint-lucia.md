PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Saint Lucia Citizenship by Investment
Citizenship of Saint Lucia can be obtained through a contribution to the National Economic Fund (NEF), the
purchase of a National Action Bond (NAB), or an investment in an approved real estate or enterprise project, with
qualifying family members included ina single application. The programme is administered by the Citizenship by
Investment Unit (CIU), and applications are submitted through authorised agents.

OPTIONS 1 AND 2
National Economic Fund and National Action Bond
The National Economic Fund (NEF) is a government fund that finances projects benefiting Saint Lucia’s economy.
The National Action Bond (NAB) is a bond issued by the Government of Saint Lucia. Both routes are made through
an authorised agent.
