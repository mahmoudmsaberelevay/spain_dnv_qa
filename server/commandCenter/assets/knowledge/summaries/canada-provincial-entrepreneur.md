## Program Overview

Canada provincial entrepreneur streams

Five Canadian provinces run entrepreneur streams for foreign business owners who run a business in the province. This summary compares the streams and gives one page for each province. A province selects and nominates; the federal stage follows. Intake in several streams is by invitation or selection.

| Figure | Detail |
|---|---|
| 5 provinces covered | British Columbia, Alberta, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador |
| Mandatory stay in the province | Every stream requires running the business there |
| CAD 100,000 | Lowest minimum investment. Varies by stream and location |
| About 13 months | Federal stage only IRCC, after nomination; not the total time |

### A stay in the province is mandatory

In all five streams the entrepreneur must come to the province, run the business there and meet the province’s performance conditions before a nomination can follow. This is a stay requirement, not an optional step. The conditions differ by province and are set out on the province pages.

Federal processing time. IRCC reports about 13 months for non-Express Entry provincial nominee applications (as at 9 October 2026). Times change monthly and are not a guarantee. This is the federal stage after nomination, not the total time.

“CAD” is the Canadian dollar. “CLB” is the Canadian Language Benchmarks (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada, canada.ca). “IRCC” is Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.

## Who Can be Included

Under the federal Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations, the family members who can be included are your spouse or common-law partner (a partner you have lived with in a conjugal relationship for at least one year); your dependent children, and those of your spouse or common-law partner (a child under 22 who is not a spouse or common-law partner, or a child aged 22 or older who has depended on a parent’s financial support since before turning 22 and cannot support themselves because of a physical or mental condition); and the dependent children of a dependent child. Each province may add its own conditions, and other relatives are not covered by these definitions. Your Elevay consultant will confirm who qualifies in your case.

Nova Scotia Entrepreneur Stream: A spouse or common-law partner who immigrates with you must show language ability of at least CLB 4. All dependants must be included in the application, even those who will not immigrate with you.

Family definitions: Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations (SOR/2002-227), s. 1 and s. 2 ([laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-2002-227/page-1.html](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-2002-227/page-1.html)), read 20 October 2026.

## Investment routes

Each province sets its own definitions and further conditions; see the province pages. Nothing in this summary guarantees an invitation, a nomination or an approval.

### British Columbia Entrepreneur Immigration — Base Stream

OPEN by invitation. Latest draw 22 September 2026: Base 10 invitations, minimum score 119.

- Net worth: at least CAD 600,000.
- Investment: eligible personal investment of at least CAD 200,000 in the business.
- Experience: at least 3 years as a business owner-manager in the last 10 years (an eligible education credential can substitute).
- Jobs: at least 1 new full-time job for a Canadian citizen or permanent resident.
- Language: CLB 4.

#### A stay in British Columbia is mandatory

Base stream: after approval the applicant signs a Performance Agreement and receives a Letter of Support for a work permit (apply within 90 days). The applicant must arrive in British Columbia within 12 months of receiving the work permit, then has up to 20 months to implement the plan and actively manage the business. A final report is submitted 18 to 20 months after arrival. Nomination follows only if the Agreement is met.

#### Processing time

British Columbia reports that around 80% of cases are processed within: registration score notification 6 weeks; application decision (work permit stage) 4 months; application decision (final report stage) 4 months; request for review 6 months. These are British Columbia’s estimates, not guarantees.

Sources: WelcomeBC, Province of British Columbia: [welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/for-entrepreneurs-and-businesses](https://www.welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/for-entrepreneurs-and-businesses) (page header last updated 10 September 2025) and invitations to apply ([welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/about-the-bc-provincial-nominee-program/invitations-to-apply](https://www.welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/about-the-bc-provincial-nominee-program/invitations-to-apply)). Read 20 October 2026.

### British Columbia Entrepreneur Immigration — Regional Stream

OPEN by invitation. Same draw: fewer than 5 invitations, minimum score 119.

- Net worth: at least CAD 300,000.
- Investment: eligible investment of at least CAD 100,000.
- Community: business outside the Metro Vancouver Regional District, aligned with a participating community’s priorities, with a completed community referral form and an exploratory visit.
- Experience: at least 3 years as an owner-manager in the last 5 years.
- Jobs: at least 1 new full-time job.
- Language: CLB 4.

The arrival and performance timeline for the Regional stream is not stated in this summary.

### Alberta Rural Entrepreneur Stream

STATUS OPEN. Alberta immigration updates page (12 August 2026): eligibility criteria unchanged; no intake pause found.

- Net worth: CAD 300,000 minimum.
- Investment: CAD 100,000 from own (and/or spouse or partner) equity.
- Language level: CLB 4.
- Ownership: At least 51% for a new business, or 100% for a business succession.
- Experience: At least 3 years as an active owner/manager, or 4 years as a senior manager, within the last 10 years.
- Community: A rural community of under 100,000 people, outside the Calgary and Edmonton areas.
- Before applying: An exploratory visit and a Community Support Letter come before the Expression of Interest.

#### A stay in Alberta is mandatory

Approved applicants sign a Business Performance Agreement and receive a Letter of Support for a two-year work permit. To be nominated, the applicant must operate the business in the rural community for at least 12 months and meet the Agreement, and show it is generating products or services after one year. After nomination: apply to IRCC within 6 months.

#### Processing time

Alberta does not publish a processing time for the Entrepreneur streams. It states that timelines vary as each file moves through several stages of review.

Sources: Government of Alberta: [alberta.ca/aaip-updates](https://www.alberta.ca/aaip-updates), [alberta.ca/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-eligibility](https://www.alberta.ca/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-eligibility), [alberta.ca/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-how-to-apply](https://www.alberta.ca/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-how-to-apply), [alberta.ca/aaip-processing-information](https://www.alberta.ca/aaip-processing-information) (updated 7 October 2026). Read 20 October 2026.

### Nova Scotia Entrepreneur Stream

STATUS ACTIVE, by invitation only (page updated 6 August 2026). Recent invitation rounds were not read, and an invitation is never guaranteed.

- Net worth: CAD 600,000 minimum; CAD 400,000 outside Halifax Regional Municipality.
- Own investment: CAD 150,000 minimum; CAD 100,000 outside Halifax Regional Municipality.
- Language level: CLB 5.
- Experience: At least 3 years running a business (one third ownership minimum), or more than 5 years in senior management.
- Age: 21 or over.
- Family: A spouse or common-law partner who immigrates with you must show language ability of at least CLB 4. All dependants must be included in the application, even those who will not immigrate with you.

#### A stay in Nova Scotia is mandatory

The entrepreneur must live permanently in Nova Scotia and actively manage the business day to day. After operating the business for a year, the entrepreneur may be nominated.

#### Processing time

Nova Scotia does not publish a processing time for the Entrepreneur Stream. Its guide states it cannot say how long assessment will take, and it cannot provide a timeline for selecting expressions of interest.

Sources: Nova Scotia Provincial Nominee Program: [liveinnovascotia.com/entrepreneur](https://liveinnovascotia.com/entrepreneur) (updated 6 August 2026) and the Entrepreneur Stream guide ([liveinnovascotia.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/Guide-NSNP-ENT-English.pdf](https://liveinnovascotia.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/Guide-NSNP-ENT-English.pdf)). Read 20 October 2026.

### New Brunswick Business Immigration

STATUS ACTIVE. The official page calls it the current and active programme; no pause notice was found. The page and guide (March 2025) carry no intake date.

- Personal net worth: CAD 500,000 minimum; CAD 300,000 for agriculture.
- Eligible investment: CAD 150,000 minimum.
- New full-time job: 1+. The plan must create at least 1.
- Business: Run the business and take part in daily management.
- Ownership or role: Own 51% or more, or be a senior manager.
- Employees: Supervise at least 2 employees.
- Language level: Not covered in this summary; your Elevay consultant will confirm.

#### A stay in New Brunswick is mandatory

Business Establishment Period of 24 months from arrival: the business must open within 9 months of arrival; the entrepreneur must operate it for 1 year within the 24 months; live in New Brunswick for 75% of the period and within 100 km of the business. A nomination can be requested after at least 6 consecutive months of operating. Report of Arrival due within 1 month of arrival; Business Performance Agreement signed within 60 days of the work permit support letter. (From the March 2025 stream guide.)

#### Processing time

New Brunswick does not publish a processing time for assessment or the nomination decision.

Sources: Government of New Brunswick: [gnb.ca/en/topic/family-home-community/immigration/provincial-nominee-program/business-immigration-stream.html](https://www.gnb.ca/en/topic/family-home-community/immigration/provincial-nominee-program/business-immigration-stream.html) and stream guide, March 2025 ([gnb.ca/content/dam/GNB3/t/fhc-fmc/immigration/docs/nb-business-immigration-stream-guide.pdf](https://www.gnb.ca/content/dam/GNB3/t/fhc-fmc/immigration/docs/nb-business-immigration-stream-guide.pdf)). Read 20 October 2026.

### Newfoundland and Labrador International Entrepreneur

STATUS OPEN. Official page (updated 4 May 2026): the Expression of Interest system is currently open.

- Assets: CAD 600,000. Personal and business assets that can be transferred to Canada.
- Investment: CAD 200,000 in a business (at least 33.3% ownership), or CAD 1 million in equity.
- Language level: CLB 5.
- Age: 21 to 59.
- Experience: 2 or more years of business ownership and management (25% or more ownership) in the last 5 years, or 5 or more years in senior management in the last 10 years.
- Jobs: At least 1 full-time job for a citizen or permanent resident.
- Exploratory visit: Required when applying from outside the province.
- Not eligible: Remote, passive-investment and property-rental businesses.

#### A stay in Newfoundland and Labrador is mandatory

Start or buy a business in the province, actively manage it every day and run it for at least one full year. If the requirements are met, the applicant may be nominated for permanent residence.

#### Processing time

Newfoundland and Labrador states it does not currently have standard processing times for its provincial nominee program.

Sources: Government of Newfoundland and Labrador: [gov.nl.ca/immigration](https://www.gov.nl.ca/immigration) (International Entrepreneur overview and eligibility criteria pages, and the candidate FAQs updated 25 September 2026). Read 20 October 2026.
