PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Canada provincial entrepreneur streams
Five Canadian provinces run entrepreneur streams for foreign business owners who run a business in the province. This
summary compares the streams and gives one page for each province. A province selects and nominates; the federal
stage follows. Intake in several streams is by invitation or selection.

A STAY IN THE PROVINCE IS MANDATORY
In all five streams the entrepreneur must come to the province, run the business there and meet the province's performance
conditions before a nomination can follow. This is a stay requirement, not an optional step. The conditions differ by province
and are set out on the province pages.
WHO CAN BE INCLUDED IN THE APPLICATION
Under the federal Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations, the family members who can be included are your spouse or
common-law partner (a partner you have lived with in a conjugal relationship for at least one year); your dependent children, and those
of your spouse or common-law partner (a child under 22 who is not a spouse or common-law partner, or a child aged 22 or older who has
depended ona parent's financial support since before turning 22 and cannot support themselves because of a physical or mental
condition); and the dependent children of a dependent child. Each province may add its own conditions, and other relatives are not
covered by these definitions. Your Elevay consultant will confirm who qualifies in your case.

A STAY IN BRITISH COLUMBIA IS MANDATORY
Base stream: after approval the applicant signs a Performance Agreement and receives a Letter of Support for a work
permit (apply within 90 days). The applicant must arrive in British Columbia within 12 months of receiving the work
permit, then has up to 20 months to implement the plan and actively manage the business. A final report is submitted
18 to 20 months after arrival. Nomination follows only if the Agreement is met. The arrival and performance timeline
for the Regional stream is not stated in this summary.
PROCESSING TIME
British Columbia reports that around 80% of cases are processed within: registration score notification 6 weeks;
application decision (work permit stage) 4 months; application decision (final report stage) 4 months; request for review 6
months. These are British Columbia's estimates, not guarantees.
IRCC reports about 13 months for non-Express Entry provincial nominee applications (as at 9 October 2026). Times
change monthly and are not a guarantee. This is the federal stage after nomination, not the total time.
All fees shall be presented on proposal stage. Nothing in this summary guarantees an invitation, a nomination or an approval.

A STAY IN ALBERTA 1S MANDATORY
Approved applicants sign a Business Performance Agreement and receive a Letter of Support for a two-year work
permit. To be nominated, the applicant must operate the business in the rural community for at least 12 months and
meet the Agreement, and show it is generating products or services after one year. After nomination: apply to IRCC
within 6 months.

A STAY IN NEW BRUNSWICK IS MANDATORY

entrepreneur must operate it for 1 year within the 24 months; live in New Brunswick for 75% of the period and within

support letter. (From the March 2025 stream guide.)
