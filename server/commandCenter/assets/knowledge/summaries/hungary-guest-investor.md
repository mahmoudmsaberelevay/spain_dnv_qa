## Program Overview

Hungary Guest Investor Residence Permit

The Hungary Guest Investor Residence Permit is a residence permit under Act XC of 2023. It is open to applicants who make one of two qualifying investments: a fund share held for 5 years, or a financial donation to a qualifying higher education institution. It is a residence permit. It does not give Hungarian or European Union citizenship.

Programme status: OPEN, checked against the in-force text of the Act on 11 October 2026.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Fund share | EUR 250,000. Held directly and continuously for at least 5 years. |
| Donation | EUR 1,000,000. To a qualifying higher education institution. |
| Permit validity | Up to 10 years. Extendable once for up to 10 more years. |
| Statutory period | 21 days. The authority’s administrative period. |

The 21-day period is the official statutory administrative period. It excludes time to submit investment proof, fix deficiencies and complete other procedural steps. It is not a promise of timing. Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved or that a permit will be extended.

### Good to know

- The permit allows the holder to run a business or work for others.
- Investment changes must be reported within 5 days.
- Accommodation in Hungary is required.
- All fees shall be presented on proposal stage.

### How the application works

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Guest investor visa | Valid for 6 months (Art. 16(5)). |
| Filing the permit application | Inside Hungary, within 30 days of first legal entry, for visa holders and visa-exempt nationals. Online through Enter Hungary, or in person at the regional directorate. |
| Appearance in person | Required for fingerprints and a photograph, even if the application is filed online. |
| Proof of investment | Attached within the visa validity, or within 6 months of first entry for visa-exempt nationals (Art. 22(7)). |
| Changes | Changes to the investment must be reported within 5 days (Art. 22(12)). |
| Statutory period | 21 days. It excludes time to submit investment proof, fix deficiencies and complete other procedural steps. Not a promise of timing. |
| Refusal | A refusal can be appealed within 8 days of delivery. |

### Permit validity and extension

The permit is valid for up to 10 years and can be extended once, for up to a further 10 years, for the same purpose (Art. 22(6)).

- Fund route: the fund share must still be held when the extension is requested.
- The extension must be requested at least 30 days before expiry, inside Hungary, through Enter Hungary.
- Extension is requested, not automatic.

### Work, business and presence in Hungary

The holder can run a business or be an executive officer of a company, and can work for others without restriction (Art. 22(1) and (2)).

Under Act XC of 2023 the guest investor permit entitles its holder to stay in Hungary (Art. 22(1)). Conditions: a qualifying investment held for the legal period, accommodation in Hungary, and reporting of changes within 5 days (Art. 22, Art. 17). The Act sets no minimum stay requirement (Art. 22 contains no such rule, and absence from Hungary is not a ground for refusal or withdrawal under Art. 226-227).

## Who Can be Included

Eligible family members can receive a family reunification permit, valid up to 10 years and renewable by up to 10 years. It is linked to the principal permit and cannot outlast it. It also allows work (Art. 73(1)(d)).

- Spouse.
- The couple’s joint minor children, including adopted and foster children.
- The holder’s dependent minor children over whom the holder has parental custody.
- The spouse’s dependent minor children over whom the spouse has parental custody.

Adult children and parents are not listed in the Act (Art. 71(2)).

Source: Act XC of 2023 ([njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2023-90-00-00](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2023-90-00-00)); accessed 11 October 2026.

## Investment routes

The two qualifying investments

The applicant chooses one of two routes. Both are set out in the Act.

### Route 1: Fund share

At least EUR 250,000

A share in a real estate fund registered by the Hungarian National Bank, held directly and continuously for at least 5 years.

#### Fund route: conditions in the Act

- The fund must have at least 40% of its net assets in Hungarian residential property.
- The fund manager must be on the register of qualified market operators (Act XXX of 2016) and meet the asset thresholds set for its type of manager.
- The fund share is held in a securities sub-account that is blocked for 5 years.
- The certificate of the fund share must be issued in the applicant’s name.

### Route 2: Donation

At least EUR 1,000,000

A financial donation for education, scientific research or artistic creation to a higher education institution maintained by a public-trust foundation with a public-service mission.

- The university confirms in a certificate that it received the donation of at least EUR 1,000,000.
- When the permit is extended, the donation route does not require a further investment.
