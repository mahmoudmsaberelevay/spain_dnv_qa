PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Hungary Guest Investor Residence Permit
The Hungary Guest Investor Residence Permit is a residence permit under Act XC of 2023. It is open to applicants
who make one of two qualifying investments: a fund share held for 5 years, or a financial donation to a qualifying
higher education institution. It is aresidence permit. It does not give Hungarian or European Union citizenship.

FUND ROUTE: CONDITIONS IN THE ACT
The fund must have at least 40% of its net assets in Hungarian residential property.
The fund manager must be on the register of qualified market operators (Act XXX of 2016) and meet the asset thresholds set
for its type of manager.
The fund share is held in a securities sub-account that is blocked for 5 years.
The certificate of the fund share must be issued in the applicant’s name.
DONATION ROUTE
The university confirms in a certificate that it received the donation of at least EUR 1,000,000.
When the permit is extended, the donation route does not require a further investment.
FEES
All fees shall be presented on proposal stage.

APPLICATION AND TIMING
How the application works
Guest investor visa Valid for 6 months (Art. 16(5)).
Filing the permit application Inside Hungary, within 30 days of first legal entry, for visa holders and visa-
exempt nationals. Online through Enter Hungary, or in person at the regional
directorate.
Appearance in person Required for fingerprints and a photograph, even if the application is filed online.
Proof of investment Attached within the visa validity, or within 6 months of first entry for visa-
exempt nationals (Art. 22(7)).
Changes Changes to the investment must be reported within 5 days (Art. 22(12)).
Statutory period 21 days. It excludes time to submit investment proof, fix deficiencies and
complete other procedural steps. Not a promise of timing.
Refusal A refusal can be appealed within 8 days of delivery.

RIGHTS, FAMILY AND PRESENCE
What the permit allows, and who can join
Work and business The holder can runa business or be an executive officer of a company, and can work
for others without restriction (Art. 22(1) and (2)).
Family permit Eligible family members can receive a family reunification permit, valid up to 10
years and renewable by up to 10 years. It is linked to the principal permit and
cannot outlast it. It also allows work (Art. 73(1)(d)).
Eligible family members Spouse; the couple’s joint minor children (including adopted and foster); the
holder’s dependent minor children under the holder’s custody; the spouse's
dependent minor children under the spouse's custody (Art. 71(2)). Adult children
and parents are not listed in the Act.
PRESENCE IN HUNGARY
Under Act XC of 2023 the guest investor permit entitles its holder to stay in Hungary (Art. 22(1)). Conditions: a
qualifying investment held for the legal period, accommodation in Hungary, and reporting of changes within 5 days (Art.
22, Art. 17). The Act sets no minimum stay requirement (Art. 22 contains no such rule, and absence from Hungary is not
a ground for refusal or withdrawal under Art. 226-227).
Sources: Act XC of 2023 (njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2023-90-00-00), Art. 22, 71(2), 73(1)(d), 17 and 226 to 227; accessed 11 October 2026.
