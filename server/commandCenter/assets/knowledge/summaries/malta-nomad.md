## Program Overview

Malta Nomad Residence Permit

The Malta Nomad Residence Permit is for people who can work remotely. It is open to non-European Union (EU), non-European Economic Area (EEA) and non-Swiss nationals aged 18 or over who work for an employer abroad, run a business abroad or freelance for clients abroad. The initial permit is valid for 1 year. The programme is run by Residency Malta (the Agency).

The Agency expects about 30 working days from receipt of payment, not counting visa processing. This is an expectation, not a guarantee. Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved.

The initial permit is valid for 1 year and can be renewed 3 times, for a maximum total stay of 4 years, at the discretion of Residency Malta and if the criteria are still met. Renewal is never guaranteed.

To renew, show a bank statement with payment transactions made in Malta, proving a cumulative period of at least 5 months in Malta over the previous 12 months. The Agency states no other yearly minimum. The second and third renewals need a declaration that the main applicant complies with all applicable tax laws. Apply 2 to 3 months before expiry. If renewal is refused or not filed, a new application can be made 12 months after expiry.

The permit does not lead to long-term residence or citizenship. Permit holders cannot work for Malta-based employers or companies. Anyone who wants to must give up the Nomad permit and apply for a Single Permit. Holding the permit does not by itself make someone subject to Maltese tax. The Agency does not give tax advice; independent advice is recommended.

## Who Can be Included

Spouse.

Minor children.

Unmarried adult children who are mainly dependent on the applicant.

Adult children unable to cope independently because of a medical condition or disability.

Dependants are added at application or at renewal; newborns are the exception.

Applicants are non-EU, non-EEA and non-Swiss nationals aged 18 or over who can work remotely in one of three ways and meet the minimum gross income.

| No. | Way of working | What it means |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Employee | Employed by an employer registered abroad, with an employment contract. |
| 2 | Business owner | Running, or being a partner or shareholder in, a business for a company registered abroad. |
| 3 | Freelancer or consultant | Working for clients with a permanent establishment abroad, with contracts. |

Minimum gross income: EUR 42,000 a year, guaranteed for at least 5 months from the application date.

A valid travel document is required.

Also required:

- A property rental or purchase agreement covering the permit period.
- Health insurance covering the EU (including Malta) and the UK, as the Agency requires, with minimum cover of EUR 100,000. It must be pre-paid for 1 full year; travel insurance is not accepted.
- A police conduct certificate and a background check.

Not eligible:

- People contracted by a foreign company to serve its Maltese subsidiary, or serving Malta-based companies or individuals.
- Nationals of, or people with close ties to, Afghanistan, North Korea, Iran, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Yemen and Venezuela. Applications from the Russian Federation and Belarus are currently not eligible either. The Agency may revise this list.

Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved.

Your Elevay consultant confirms which family members can be included and prepares the document list (see the separate Document Checklist).
