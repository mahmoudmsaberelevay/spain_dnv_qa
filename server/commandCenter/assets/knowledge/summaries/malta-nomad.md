PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Malta Nomad Residence Permit
The Malta Nomad Residence Permit is for people who can work remotely. It is open to non-European Union (EU),
non-European Economic Area (EEA) and non-Swiss nationals aged 18 or over who work for an employer abroad,
run a business abroad or freelance for clients abroad. The initial permit is valid for 1 year. The programme is run by
Residency Malta (the Agency).

ELIGIBILITY AND INCOME
Who can apply, and the income to show
Applicants are non-EU, non-EEA and non-Swiss nationals aged 18 or over who can work remotely in one of three
ways and meet the minimum gross income.

PERMIT AND RENEWAL
Permit validity, renewal and time in Malta
Initial permit Valid for 1 year.
Renewal Can be renewed 3 times, for a maximum total stay of 4 years, at the discretion of
Residency Malta and if the criteria are still met. Renewal is never guaranteed.
Time in Malta To renew, show a bank statement with payment transactions made in Malta, proving a
cumulative period of at least 5 months in Malta over the previous 12 months. The
Agency states no other yearly minimum.
Tax declaration The second and third renewals need a declaration that the main applicant complies
with all applicable tax laws.
When to apply 2 to 3 months before expiry.
If renewal is refused or not Anew application can be made 12 months after expiry.
