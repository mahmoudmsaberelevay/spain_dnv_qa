or in one of several other approved routes, and family members can be included for a fee of EUR 100 per person.

OPTIONAL TAX REGIME: NON-DOM LUMP SUM (ARTICLE 5A)
This is a Greek tax regime and is not part of the residence permit. It requires becoming a Greek tax resident, which the
permit itself does not require. It is an option that may suit some clients; eligibility is not promised.
Lump-sum tax EUR 100,000 per tax year on foreign income, for up to 15 tax years
Relatives EUR 20,000 for each relative included (spouse, direct-line ascendants or descendants)
Investment condition An investment of at least EUR 500,000 in Greek real estate, businesses, securities or
stakes within 3 years of the application
Tax residence history Not a Greek tax resident in 7 of the previous 8 years
Application To the Tax Procedures and Service Center (KEFODE) by 31 March
Inheritance and gifts Foreign inheritances and gifts of movable property abroad are exempt from Greek
inheritance and gift tax while in the regime
PLEASE NOTE
This is general information, not individual tax advice. Please obtain independent advice from a qualified tax adviser
before you decide.
