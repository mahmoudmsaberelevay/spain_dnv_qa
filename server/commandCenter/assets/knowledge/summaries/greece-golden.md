## Program Overview

Greece Residency by Investment

A qualifying investment in Greece can lead to a Greek residence permit. The investment can be made in real estate or in one of several other approved routes, and family members can be included for a fee of EUR 100 per person.

### Optional tax regime: non-dom lump sum (Article 5A)

This is a Greek tax regime and is not part of the residence permit. It requires becoming a Greek tax resident, which the permit itself does not require. It is an option that may suit some clients; eligibility is not promised.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Lump-sum tax | EUR 100,000 per tax year on foreign income, for up to 15 tax years |
| Relatives | EUR 20,000 for each relative included (spouse, direct-line ascendants or descendants) |
| Investment condition | An investment of at least EUR 500,000 in Greek real estate, businesses, securities or stakes within 3 years of the application |
| Tax residence history | Not a Greek tax resident in 7 of the previous 8 years |
| Application | To the Tax Procedures and Service Center (KEFODE) by 31 March |
| Inheritance and gifts | Foreign inheritances and gifts of movable property abroad are exempt from Greek inheritance and gift tax while in the regime |

PLEASE NOTE

This is general information, not individual tax advice. Please obtain independent advice from a qualified tax adviser before you decide.

## Investment routes

Besides real estate, Greece offers several other investment routes.

| Minimum investment | Investment |
|---|---|
| EUR 800,000 | Real estate in Attica, Thessaloniki regional unit, Mykonos, Santorini and islands with more than 3,100 people |
| EUR 400,000 | Real estate everywhere else in Greece |
| EUR 250,000 | Change of use of a non-residential property, or a listed building restored or reconstructed |
| EUR 800,000 or EUR 400,000, depending on the area | Tourist accommodation or timeshare |
| EUR 800,000 | Greek-market listed shares, corporate bonds or Greek government bonds (through a Greek intermediary or bank) |
| EUR 500,000 | Capital increase shares or bonds of a Greek company listed on a regulated market or a multilateral trading facility (MTF); Capital increase of a Public Limited Real Estate Investment Company (REIC); BPCC/MFBP [full names awaiting Elevay confirmation] or Alternative Investment Fund (AIF) / VCC [full name awaiting Elevay confirmation] investing only in Greek enterprises; Greek government bonds (at least 3 years remaining); Time deposit in a Greek bank (at least 1 year); Establishing a business |
| EUR 350,000 | Alternative Investment Fund (AIF) units or shares investing only in Greece |
| EUR 250,000 | Registered start-up (maximum 33% stake, at least 2 jobs, held 5 years) |

The residence permit is granted for 5 years and is renewable while the property is not sold.

Each route has its own conditions. Your Elevay consultant will confirm the route that suits your situation and the current requirements before you invest. Fees and requirements may change.
