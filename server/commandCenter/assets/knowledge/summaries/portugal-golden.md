A.A qualifying investment, kept for at least 5 years, allows the investor and eligible family members to apply for a

THE PROGRAMME
Investment routes
Six routes qualify. Each investment must be kept for at least 5 years. Amounts are the minimum for each route.
1 Job creation. Create at least 10 jobs. No investment
amount
2 Scientific research. Transfer capital into scientific research institutions. EUR 500,000
3 Culture. Transfer capital into artistic production, or the recovery or maintenance of EUR 250,000
national cultural heritage, through listed public or public-interest bodies.
4 Venture capital or company-capitalisation funds. Buy fund units with at least a 5-year EUR 500,000
maturity, with at least 60% invested in companies based in Portugal.
5 Collective investment undertakings. Buy units in non-real-estate funds with at least a 5- EUR 500,000
year maturity, with at least 60% invested in companies based in Portugal.
6 Company set-up. Set up a company in Portugal with 5 permanent jobs, or make a capital EUR 500,000
increase with 5 permanent jobs, or keep 10 jobs (at least 5 permanent) for 3 years.
LOW-DENSITY TERRITORIES
If the investment is made in a low-density territory (Portaria No. 208/2017), the minimum may be 20% lower for
three routes only. The fund routes (4 and 5) get no reduction.
1. Job creation 8 jobs
2. Scientific research EUR 400,000
3. Culture EUR 200,000
Sources: Law No. 23/2007, Articles 3 and 90-A, consolidated text including Law No. 62/2026, in force 11 September 2026 (diariodarepublica.pt),
accessed 10 October 2026. Portaria No. 208/2017 as cited in Article 3.

FAMILY, STAY AND THE ROAD AHEAD
Staying, renewing and long-term options
What the Golden Visa asks of the holder after approval, and the longer-term options that exist under Portuguese
law.
MINIMUM STAY RENEWAL
7 days per year Two-year periods
Source: Elevay management confirmation. The permit is renewed in two-year periods while an
investment requirement is kept.
FAMILY MEMBERS
Under Law No. 23/2007, Article 99, family members can include the spouse, minor or dependent children, adopted minors,
unmarried adult children who are studying, dependent first-degree parents of the investor or of the spouse, and minor
siblings under guardianship. The usual two-year wait for family reunification does not apply to the family of Golden Visa
holders (Article 98(3)(c)). AIMA assesses dependency and study conditions.
PERMANENT RESIDENCE
Permanent residence needs 5 years of temporary residence, basic Portuguese, means of subsistence and housing (Article
80). It is never guaranteed.
PORTUGUESE CITIZENSHIP
Under Organic Law No. 1/2026, in force from 19 May 2026, Portuguese nationality by residence requires 10 years of legal
residence, or 7 years for nationals of Portuguese-speaking countries and of European Union (EU) member states.
Citizenship is never guaranteed or automatic, and other conditions apply. Applications filed before 19 May 2026 keep the
earlier law.
Sources: Law No. 23/2007, Articles 80, 90-A, 98 and 99, consolidated text (diariodarepublica.pt); Organic Law No. 1/2026 (justica.gov.pt, Nationality Law
news item), accessed 10 October 2026; minimum stay: Elevay management confirmation, 10 October 2026
