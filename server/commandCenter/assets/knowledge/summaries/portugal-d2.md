PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Portugal D2 Residence Visa
The D2 is a Portuguese residence visa for people who will work for themselves or run a business in Portugal.
Elevay offers three routes: independent professional, immigrant entrepreneur and Startup Visa. A temporary
residence permit is issued by the Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum (AIMA).

MINIMUM STAY: PLEASE READ THIS CAREFULLY
The residence permit can be cancelled if you are absent from Portugal for more than 6 consecutive
months, or 8 months in total, during its 2-year validity, without justified reason. In practice, plan to
spend around 16 of the 24 months in Portugal.

APOSTILLE OR ATTESTATION: WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE?
Apostille Asingle certificate added to a public document by the competent authority of a country that is a party to
the Hague Apostille Convention (1961). Portugal is a party. For documents covered by the Convention, the
apostille is the only formality that may be required to certify the signature, the capacity of the signatory
and any seal or stamp.
Attestation Also called legalisation, a longer route: the document is authenticated by the foreign ministry (MOFA) of
the country that issued it and then by the Portuguese embassy or consulate. This is what the document
checklist means by “attested by the Portuguese embassy and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs”
Which one applies depends on the country that issued the document. Translation into Portuguese is a separate step, as the
checklist shows. Your Elevay consultant will confirm what applies to each of your documents.
PORTUGUESE CITIZENSHIP
Under Organic Law No. 1/2026, in force from 19 May 2026, Portuguese nationality by residence requires 10 years of legal residence, or 7
years for nationals of Portuguese-speaking countries and of European Union (EU) member states. Citizenship is never guaranteed or
automatic, and other conditions apply. Applications filed before 19 May 2026 keep the earlier law.
