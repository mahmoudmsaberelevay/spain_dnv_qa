## Program Overview

Portugal D2 Residence Visa

The D2 is a Portuguese residence visa for people who will work for themselves or run a business in Portugal. Elevay offers three routes: independent professional, immigrant entrepreneur and Startup Visa. A temporary residence permit is issued by the Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum (AIMA).

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Processing time | 3 to 6 months, based on Elevay’s experience. Not an official or guaranteed timeline. |
| Residence permit | 2 years, renewable in 3-year periods while the conditions are met. |
| Longest absence | 6 months in a row / 8 months in total during the 2-year permit, without justified reason. |
| Indicative means for one adult | EUR 6,440: 7 x EUR 920 (2026 minimum wage), indicative, not an official published amount. |

The indicative EUR 6,440 calculation is about 7 months of means of subsistence multiplied by the 2026 minimum wage of EUR 920 a month. How the amount was reached is explained below. Please confirm with the consulate.

Staying in Portugal and renewing

The residence permit can be cancelled if you are absent from Portugal for more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 months in total, during its 2-year validity, without justified reason. In practice, plan to spend around 16 of the 24 months in Portugal.

| Period | Detail |
|---|---|
| 6 months in a row | The longest unjustified absence |
| 8 months in total | Unjustified absence within the 2 years |
| 16 of 24 months | A planning guide, not a legal minimum |

The law sets no minimum number of days. The 16-month figure is a planning guide calculated from the legal limits. Cancellation is possible, not automatic. Absences beyond these limits must be justified to the Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum (AIMA) before you leave. The law provides that the permit is not cancelled if the holder was carrying out professional, business, cultural or social activity while away. For a permanent residence permit, the limits are 24 consecutive months or 30 months in total within three years.

| Visa and permit item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Visa | Valid for two entries and 4 months. Legal decision deadline: 60 days (Law No. 23/2007, Art. 58). |
| Residence permit | Valid for 2 years, renewable in successive 3-year periods (Art. 75(1)). |
| Renewal conditions | Means of subsistence, accommodation, tax and social security obligations met, and no conviction above 1 year (Art. 78(2)). Renewal is never guaranteed. |

The consulate or AIMA assesses the evidence for each route; approval is never guaranteed. This summary is not individual eligibility advice.

## Investment routes

Three D2 routes and the funds to show

| No. | Route | Who it is for and what must be shown |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Independent professional — Art. 60(1) | For people who will work for themselves in Portugal, such as consultants and freelancers. The applicant must show a services contract or a written proposal to provide services, plus the right to practise where the profession requires it. |
| 2 | Immigrant entrepreneur — Art. 60(2) | For people who invest in or set up a business in Portugal. Portuguese law sets no minimum investment amount for this route. The applicant must show investment operations already made, or financial means available in Portugal (including financing from a Portuguese financial institution), plus proof by any means of the intention to invest. |
| 3 | Startup Visa — Art. 89(4) | For founders whose project is accepted by a certified incubator in Portugal. A declaration from the Agency for Competitiveness and Innovation (IAPMEI) certifies the incubation contract. |

Indicative funds to show: how we reached the figure

The law asks for means of subsistence for a period not less than the maximum admissible stay. We read that as the 4-month residence visa plus a 90-day extension, which is about 7 months. We multiply 7 months by the 2026 minimum wage of EUR 920 a month, with the official shares: first adult 100%, second adult 50%, each child 30%.

| Household | Calculation | Indicative amount |
|---|---|---:|
| One adult | 7 x EUR 920 | EUR 6,440 |
| Second adult (spouse), added | 50% of EUR 6,440 | EUR 3,220 |
| Each child, added | 30% of EUR 6,440 | EUR 1,932 |
| Couple | 6,440 + 3,220 | EUR 9,660 |
| Couple plus one child | 9,660 + 1,932 | EUR 11,592 |

Indicative only. The 7-month period is our reading of the law, and these amounts are not an official published figure. Please confirm with the consulate. Meeting them does not guarantee that a visa will be granted.

Sources: Law No. 23/2007, Arts. 58(2), 60, 72(1)(c) and 89(4) (diariodarepublica.pt); Portaria No. 1563/2007, Art. 5; vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/national-visas/necessary-documentation/residency; minimum wage: dgert.gov.pt; accessed 10 October 2026.

## Passport access

Under Organic Law No. 1/2026, in force from 19 May 2026, Portuguese nationality by residence requires 10 years of legal residence, or 7 years for nationals of Portuguese-speaking countries and of European Union (EU) member states. Citizenship is never guaranteed or automatic, and other conditions apply. Applications filed before 19 May 2026 keep the earlier law.

Sources: Organic Law No. 1/2026 (justica.gov.pt, Nationality Law news item). Accessed 10 October 2026 unless stated.
