## Program Overview

Latvia investor residence

Latvia offers a temporary residence permit to foreign investors who invest in the share capital of a Latvian company. It is the only investor route offered under the new Immigration Law, in force since 15 September 2026. The permit is granted for up to 2 years and can be renewed while the conditions still exist.

Programme status: OPEN, checked against the in-force text of the Act on 11 October 2026.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Permit validity | Up to 2 years. Renewable while the conditions still exist. |
| Share capital, smaller company | EUR 50,000 |
| Share capital, larger company | EUR 100,000 |
| State budget payment | EUR 10,000. Paid when requesting the first permit. |
| Taxes paid a year | EUR 40,000 by the company; EUR 100,000 for the larger tier |

Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved or renewed.

### Good to know

- Latvia now offers the share-capital route as its investor residence route.
- The permit is valid only while the company keeps paying the required taxes each year.
- The EUR 10,000 state payment is non-refundable except in cases set by Cabinet regulation.
- All fees shall be presented on proposal stage.

### The route at a glance

| Point | What the law states |
|---|---|
| Investment | EUR 50,000 or EUR 100,000 invested in the share capital of a Latvian company, depending on the size of the company |
| State payment | EUR 10,000 to the state budget, one-off |
| Ongoing condition | The company pays at least EUR 40,000 (or EUR 100,000) in taxes in each reporting year of the permit |
| Validity | Up to 2 years, renewable while the conditions still exist |

### State payment and the permit

The state budget payment is paid to the state budget when requesting the first permit. It is one-off and non-refundable, except in cases set by Cabinet regulation (Art. 27(1)(10) and Art. 30). This is a statutory state payment, not an Elevay fee.

All fees shall be presented on proposal stage.

| Stage | What the law states |
|---|---|
| First permit | Temporary residence permit for up to 2 years (Art. 27(1)(10) and Art. 29(3)). |
| Each reporting year | The company pays the required taxes (EUR 40,000, or EUR 100,000 for the larger tier). |
| Renewal | Available while the conditions still exist. It is never guaranteed. |

### Permanent residence

As of 20 October 2026, the only permanent residence in Latvia is European Union (EU) long-term resident status. The law requires 5 years of continuous residence, sufficient means, Latvian at A2 level and a Latvian culture and history course. Permanent residence is never guaranteed.

The current version of the law is valid until 30 December 2027, so this wording may change.

### What changed in 2026

The new Immigration Law came into force on 15 September 2026. Latvia now offers the share-capital route described in this summary.

## Who Can be Included

Family members can request a temporary residence permit for the term of the investor’s permit.

- The investor’s spouse.
- The minor children of the investor or of the spouse, including children under guardianship.
- Persons under the investor’s curatorship (aizgādņība).

Family member as defined in Section 1 of the Immigration Law. Your Elevay consultant will confirm who can be included in your case.

## Investment routes

Investing in Latvian company share capital

The requirement

Invest in the share capital of a Latvian company, either in a new company or as an increase of an existing company’s capital. The amount depends on the size of the company (Immigration Law, Art. 27(1)(10)).

| Tier | Company | Investment |
|---|---|---|
| Smaller company | Up to 50 employees, and turnover or balance sheet total up to EUR 10 million. Up to 10 foreigners per company on this tier. | EUR 50,000 in share capital |
| Larger company | More than 50 employees, or turnover or balance sheet total above EUR 10 million (Latvian subsidiaries are counted). | EUR 100,000 in share capital |

The limit of 10 foreigners per company is stated in the law for the EUR 50,000 tier only.

### The ongoing condition

| Smaller company | Larger company |
|---|---|
| EUR 40,000 taxes a year, smaller company. Paid to the state and municipal budgets. | EUR 100,000 taxes a year, larger company. Taxes of Latvian subsidiaries also count. |

The permit is valid only if, in each reporting year of its validity, the company pays at least this amount in taxes. Refunded taxes are not counted.
