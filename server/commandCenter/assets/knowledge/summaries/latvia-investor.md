PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Latvia investor residence
Latvia offers a temporary residence permit to foreign investors who invest in the share capital of a Latvian
company. It is the only investor route offered under the new Immigration Law, in force since 15 September 2026.
The permit is granted for up to 2 years and can be renewed while the conditions still exist.

THE ROUTE
Investing in Latvian company share capital
THE REQUIREMENT
Invest in the share capital of a Latvian company, either in a new company or as an increase of an existing company’s
capital. The amount depends on the size of the company (Immigration Law, Art. 27(1)(10)).

FAMILY AND LONG-TERM OPTIONS
Family, permanent residence and what changed
FAMILY MEMBERS
Family members can request a temporary residence permit for the term of the investor's permit.
e The investor's spouse
e The minor children of the investor or of the spouse, including children under guardianship
e Persons under the investor's curatorship (aizgadniba)
Family member as defined in Section 1 of the Immigration Law. Your Elevay consultant will confirm who can be included in your case.
PERMANENT RESIDENCE
As of 20 October 2026, the only permanent residence in Latvia is European Union (EU) long-term resident status. The law
requires 5 years of continuous residence, sufficient means, Latvian at A2 level and a Latvian culture and history course.
Permanent residence is never guaranteed.
The current version of the law is valid until 30 December 2027, so this wording may change.
WHAT CHANGED IN 2026
The new Immigration Law came into force on 15 September 2026. Latvia now offers the share-capital route described in
this summary.
