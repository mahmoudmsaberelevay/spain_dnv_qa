Spain Entrepreneur Residence
Spain's entrepreneur residence authorisation (Articles 68 to 70 of Law 14/2013) is for non-EU nationals over 18
who want to start, run or manage an innovative business activity of special economic interest for Spain. The
programme is open. A favourable report on the project is mandatory.

there are reasonable doubts about reputational, regulatory, ethical or speculative risks.

VALIDITY AND RENEWAL
Entrepreneur visa Valid for 1 year, to make preliminary arrangements. It can convert to residence without a
new visa once the business has effectively started (Article 68).
Initial authorisation A single validity of 3 years (Ministry management criterion 2/2025, Article 69.1).
Renewal A separate route with the same three steps. The authority checks that the conditions
that justified the grant still hold, and renewals run in 2-year periods while they are met
(Article 76.2). Renewal is never promised.
PROCESSING TIME (LEGAL MAXIMUM)
Residence authorisation A maximum of 20 days from filing. If no decision is made in that time, the authorisation is
deemed granted by administrative silence (Article 76.1).
ENISA report 10 working days from the UGE’s request (Article 70). This is a separate step. The two
periods are not added together here.
Visa from abroad 10 working days, longer where the Schengen consultation applies (Article 75.5).
These are legal maximums, not promises. Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved.

FAMILY, SOCIAL SECURITY AND TAX
Family members, Social Security and tax
FAMILY MEMBERS
Spouse or partner Spouse or person in an analogous affective relationship. An unmarried, unregistered
partner shows at least 1 year of continuous cohabitation, unless there are common children.
Children Minor children, and adult children who depend economically on the holder and have not
formed their own family unit. Aged 18 to under 26: no income from work or public benefits,
and enrolled in a qualifying course or registered as a job-seeker. Aged 26 or over: only with
an incapacity that prevents them from being economically independent.
Parents and Dependent ascendants show economic or physical dependence; over 80 is presumed. Family
grandparents members may apply with the holder or later.
Family application: filed by the family member, the holder or the hiring entity; the same fee model applies to holder and dependants. These age
and dependence rules come from the UGE family guidance. {FAMDOC}
SOCIAL SECURITY BEFORE YOU START
The application includes a signed declaration committing to meet your Social Security obligations before you start any
business or professional activity in Spain. Do not start work before you are correctly registered.
TAX: SEEK YOUR OWN ADVICE
Elevay does not give tax advice. Tax residence and personal tax treatment are not set by the immigration authority. It is
very important that you seek your own independent tax advice before you apply.
Contribution amounts and tax rates are not set by the immigration authority and are not shown here.
