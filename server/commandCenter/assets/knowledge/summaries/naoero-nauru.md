## 1. Program Overview

Naoero Citizenship by Investment

The Naoero Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Program (ECRCP), established under the Naoero Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Act 2024, grants citizenship in return for a contribution that supports development projects, including climate resilience. Applications are submitted through the programme’s agents, and the main applicant can include their spouse, dependent children, parents and siblings.

There is no residency requirement. Dual citizenship is allowed: you can keep your current nationality. The main applicant must be at least 18 years old. All amounts are in US dollars.

## 2. Who Can be Included

- The spouse of the main applicant
- Dependent children
- Parents
- Siblings of the main applicant or of the spouse

The main applicant must be at least 18 years old.

### Eligibility and process

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Minimum age | The main applicant must be at least 18 years old. |
| Due diligence | Checks are run on all applicants aged 16 and above by the Program Office, the Naoero Police Force and independent firms. Applicants must be verified and approved by an approved due diligence firm. |
| Interview | Every main applicant attends an interview conducted by a due diligence firm, virtually or in person. A dependant aged 16 or above may also be asked to attend. |
| Criminal record | Applicants must not have been convicted of an offence punishable by 12 months’ imprisonment or more, and must not be under investigation or charged for such an offence. |
| Police certificate | A police certificate is required for the country of residence where the applicant lived for more than six months within the last ten years. |
| Decision | The Program Office recommends approval or rejection to the Minister, and the application then goes to Cabinet for a final decision. An applicant who does not meet every criterion cannot be approved. |
| Residency | There is no residency requirement. |

The programme was established through the Naoero Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Act 2024.

Sources: ecrcp.gov.nr (How to Apply; FAQ; About), accessed 8 October 2026.

## 3. Investment routes

One route is offered: a contribution to the Naoero programme, plus government fees that depend on the number of people in the application.

Sources: ecrcp.gov.nr (Contribution; How to Apply; About), accessed 8 October 2026.

## 4. Contribution options

| Applicant | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Main applicant | US$90,000 until 31 December 2026; US$115,000 from 1 January 2027 |
| Spouse, dependent child or parent aged 16 and above | US$2,000 each |
| Dependants under 16 | No contribution |
| Sibling of the main applicant or spouse | US$15,000 each |

The applicant makes a contribution that the government uses to fund development projects, including climate resilience initiatives and infrastructure improvements. The main applicant’s contribution is US$90,000 for applications filed before 31 December 2026, after which it is US$115,000.

The US$90,000 contribution is a limited-time offer for applications filed before 31 December 2026.

Sources: ecrcp.gov.nr (Contribution; FAQ; About), accessed 8 October 2026.

Source: Passport Index (passportindex.org), accessed 8 October 2026. The total is the sum of the three categories shown.

### What the categories mean

- Visa-free: no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- Visa on arrival: the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- eTA: an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
