PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Naoero Citizenship by Investment
The Naoero Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Program (ECRCP), established under the Naoero
Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Act 2024, grants citizenship in return for a contribution that
supports development projects, including climate resilience. Applications are submitted through the programme's
agents, and the main applicant can include their spouse, dependent children, parents and siblings.

THE PROGRAMME
Contribution and fees
One route is offered: a contribution to the Naoero programme, plus government fees that depend on the number
of people in the application.
ECONOMIC AND CLIMATE RESILIENCE CITIZENSHIP PROGRAM
Contribution
The applicant makes a contribution that the government uses to fund development projects, including climate
resilience initiatives and infrastructure improvements. The main applicant's contribution is USS90,000 for
applications filed before 31 December 2026, after which it is USS115,000.

ELIGIBILITY AND PROCESS
How the programme works
What the official programme site states about who can apply, the checks involved and the legal basis.
Minimum age The main applicant must be at least 18 years old.
Due diligence Checks are run on all applicants aged 16 and above by the Program Office, the Naoero
Police Force and independent firms. Applicants must be verified and approved by an
approved due diligence firm.
Interview Every main applicant attends an interview conducted by a due diligence firm, virtually or
in person. A dependant aged 16 or above may also be asked to attend.
Criminal record Applicants must not have been convicted of an offence punishable by 12 months’
imprisonment or more, and must not be under investigation or charged for such an
offence.
Police certificate A police certificate is required for the country of residence where the applicant lived for
more than six months within the last ten years.
Decision The Program Office recommends approval or rejection to the Minister, and the
application then goes to Cabinet for a final decision. An applicant who does not meet
every criterion cannot be approved.
Residency There is no residency requirement.
LEGAL BASIS
The programme was established through the Naoero Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Act 2024.
Sources: ecrcp.gov.nr (How to Apply; FAQ; About), accessed 8 October 2026.
