PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
UAE Golden Visa (investor)
The UAE Golden Visa for investors is a long-term residence in the United Arab Emirates (UAE) for people who
invest in the country. It is listed as open and processed by the ICP, the DLD and the GDRFA. The residence is valid
for 10 years, is renewable if the conditions are still met, and needs no sponsor. The investor categories are real
estate, a financial deposit, company capital or a partner share, and federal tax paid.

ROUTE 1: REAL ESTATE
Investing in UAE property
THE REQUIREMENT
Property or properties in the UAE with a total value of at least AED 2,000,000, fully owned and registered in the
investor's name (ICP).
Off-plan units Off-plan units bought from an approved local developer count if the total is at least AED
2,000,000 (ICP).
Joint property A share in a joint property must itself be at least AED 2,000,000 (GDRFA).
Value The value is certified by a Dubai Land Department property status certificate. A
valuation certificate from a DLD-licensed office is also accepted (GDRFA). The DLD
service description states a purchase value of AED 2,000,000 or more at the time of
purchase.
Mortgaged property Mortgaged property is accepted. DLD requires a bank letter stating the amount paid and
the balance.
Holding The property may not be disposed of during the 10-year residency, and a lien is placed on
the property for the whole residency period (GDRFA).
Renewal All the requirements for issuing the residence must be maintained when applying for
renewal (ICP).

FAMILY, INSURANCE AND PRESENCE
Family members and other conditions
FAMILY MEMBERS
Spouse, children and parents can be sponsored. ICP states children regardless of age; DLD requires an undertaking
that sons and daughters over 18 are not married.
lf the sponsor dies, sponsored family members may remain for the validity of their permits (ICP).
HEALTH INSURANCE
ICP requires the investor and family members to have comprehensive and valid health insurance: at the time of applying
for the public-investment routes, and throughout the validity period for real estate.
TIME SPENT OUTSIDE THE UAE
GDRFA Golden Residence Permit holders are exempt from the 180-day residency law. The
residency is void only if it expires outside the country.
u.ae Holders can stay outside the UAE for more than the usual six months needed to keep a
residence visa valid.
This is the authorities’ statement and is not a guarantee. It does not mean absence is unlimited.
