PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Grenada Citizenship by Investment
Citizenship of Grenada can be obtained through a contribution to the National Transformation Fund (NTF) or
through an investment in an approved real estate project, with qualifying family members included ina single
application. The programme is administered by the Investment Migration Agency (IMA) Grenada, and applications
are submitted through authorised agents.

OPTION 1
National Transformation Fund
The National Transformation Fund (NTF) is a government fund that finances projects benefiting Grenada’s
economy and its diversification. Applicants make a one-time contribution through an authorised local agent.
Under this route, applicants may apply for citizenship immediately, or first apply for permanent residence and
apply for citizenship later.

INELIGIBLE AND RESTRICTED NATIONALITIES
Nationals residing in Iran, North Korea, Russia, Belarus, Sudan, Yemen and Afghanistan are ineligible, without
exception.
Nationals of Iran, Sudan, Yemen and Afghanistan may apply only if they: (a) migrated before the age of majority
and/or have held permanent residence or a valid visa in Canada, the United Kingdom, a Schengen state, the United
States, Australia, New Zealand, Saudi Arabia or the United Arab Emirates for at least 10 years; (b) keep no economic
ties to any restricted country; and (c) accept that enhanced due diligence may be carried out in the country of birth,
at a significantly higher cost.
All application, processing and due diligence fees are non-refundable. Elevay professional fees are provided upon request. All government and
Elevay fees are subject to change without notice

OPTIONS 2 AND 3
Real Estate Investment
Citizenship can also be obtained by investing in a government-approved project, mostly luxury hotels, resorts and
villas in Grenada. Investment is permitted only in projects approved by the Minister and published in the Gazette.
The investment must be made through an authorised local agent.
