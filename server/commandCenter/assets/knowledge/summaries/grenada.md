## 1. Program Overview

Grenada Citizenship by Investment

Citizenship of Grenada can be obtained through a contribution to the National Transformation Fund (NTF) or through an investment in an approved real estate project, with qualifying family members included in a single application. The programme is administered by the Investment Migration Agency (IMA) Grenada, and applications are submitted through authorised agents.

| Information | Detail |

## 2. Who Can be Included

- The spouse of the main applicant
- Children under 18, and children aged 18 to under 30 who are supported by the main applicant
- Children aged 18 or over who are physically or mentally challenged, live with and are fully supported by the main applicant
- A parent or grandparent of the main applicant or spouse who is fully supported by the main applicant
- An unmarried sibling of the main applicant or spouse, aged 18 or over, with no children
- A child born within 12 months of the grant of citizenship

### GOOD TO KNOW

- The main applicant must be at least 18 years old, in good health and able to show the source of funds is legal.
- A mandatory online interview forms part of due diligence. Applications go through an authorised agent.
- Some nationalities are ineligible or restricted: see page 3.
- Grenada allows dual citizenship. All fees are non-refundable.

### INELIGIBLE AND RESTRICTED NATIONALITIES

Nationals residing in Iran, North Korea, Russia, Belarus, Sudan, Yemen and Afghanistan are ineligible, without exception.

Nationals of Iran, Sudan, Yemen and Afghanistan may apply only if they: (a) migrated before the age of majority and/or have held permanent residence or a valid visa in Canada, the United Kingdom, a Schengen state, the United States, Australia, New Zealand, Saudi Arabia or the United Arab Emirates for at least 10 years; (b) keep no economic ties to any restricted country; and (c) accept that enhanced due diligence may be carried out in the country of birth, at a significantly higher cost.

## 3. Investment routes

### INVESTMENT ROUTES AT A GLANCE

| Route | Minimum investment | Holding period |
|---|---|---|
| Option 1: National Transformation Fund (NTF) | US$235,000 (main applicant plus up to three dependants) | N/A |
| Option 2: Approved real estate (single owner) | US$350,000, plus a US$50,000 government contribution | 5 years |
| Option 3: Approved real estate (shared tourism accommodation unit) | US$270,000 per share, plus a US$50,000 government contribution | 5 years |

Sources: imagrenada.gd (incl. Circulars 1 and 3 of 2024), Act No. 3 of 2019, S.R.O. 12 of 2024, accessed 7 October 2026.

### OPTIONS 2 AND 3

### Real Estate Investment

Citizenship can also be obtained by investing in a government-approved project, mostly luxury hotels, resorts and villas in Grenada. Investment is permitted only in projects approved by the Minister and published in the Gazette. The investment must be made through an authorised local agent.

### OPTION 2

### Approved real estate

A minimum investment of US$350,000 in an approved project, plus a US$50,000 government contribution for the main applicant and up to three dependants.

### OPTION 3

### Shared tourism accommodation unit

A minimum investment of US$270,000 per share in an approved tourism accommodation unit bought jointly by two or more investors, plus a US$50,000 government contribution for the main applicant and up to three dependants.

### HOLDING PERIOD

A person granted citizenship may not dispose of the investment until five years after citizenship is granted.

Sources: imagrenada.gd (Becoming a Citizen; Approved Projects; Circulars 2 and 3 of 2024), Act No. 3 of 2019, S.R.O. 12 of 2024, accessed 7 October 2026.

## 4. Contribution options

### OPTION 1

### National Transformation Fund

The National Transformation Fund (NTF) is a government fund that finances projects benefiting Grenada’s economy and its diversification. Applicants make a one-time contribution through an authorised local agent. Under this route, applicants may apply for citizenship immediately, or first apply for permanent residence and apply for citizenship later.

### OPTION 1

### Contribution

US$235,000 for a single applicant, or for a main applicant with up to three dependants. This is a one-time, non-refundable contribution.

### OPTION 1

### Additional dependants

Each dependant beyond the main applicant plus three: US$25,000; parent or grandparent aged 55 or under: US$50,000; sibling: US$75,000.

### WHAT THE CATEGORIES MEAN

- Visa-free: no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- Visa on arrival: the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- eTA: an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
