## 1. Program Overview

Citizenship of Antigua & Barbuda can be obtained through a contribution (Option 1 or 2), an approved real estate investment (Option 3) or an approved business investment (Option 4), with qualifying family members included in a single application. The programme is administered by the Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU).

- **9–12 months\*:** Typical time to citizenship, based on current Elevay files.
- **5 days:** Minimum stay required in any five-year period after citizenship.
- **5 years:** Minimum holding period for the real estate route.
- **144†:** Visa-free, visa-on-arrival and eTA destinations.

\* Based on Elevay’s experience processing current files. This is not an official or guaranteed timeline and varies by case.

† Passport Index, accessed 7 October 2026. Indicative only: see the passport access page for the breakdown, sources and important notice.

## 2. Who Can Be Included

- The spouse of the main applicant
- Children aged 0 to 30 who are financially dependent on the main applicant
- Children aged 18 or over who are disabled, live with and are fully supported by the main applicant
- A parent or grandparent of the main applicant or spouse, aged 55 or over and financially dependent on the main applicant
- An unmarried sibling of the main applicant or spouse

## 3. Investment Routes

| Route | Minimum investment | Holding period |
|---|---|---|
| Option 1: National Development Fund (NDF) | US$230,000 per application | N/A |
| Option 2: University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund | US$260,000 (minimum of six persons per application) | N/A |
| Option 3: Approved real estate | US$300,000 | 5 years |
| Option 4: Approved business investment | US$1,500,000, or US$5,000,000 jointly (each investor at least US$400,000) | Not stated by the CIU |

### Option 3 — Approved Real Estate

Property valued at a minimum of US$300,000 in an approved project, supported by a binding purchase agreement. The property cannot be re-sold until 5 years after purchase, unless an alternate officially approved property in Antigua and Barbuda is purchased.

### Option 4 — Approved Business

An investment of at least US$1,500,000 in an approved business by a single applicant, or a joint investment of at least US$5,000,000 by two or more persons, each contributing at least US$400,000.

## 4. Contribution Options

Two routes involve a contribution to a government fund: the National Development Fund (NDF) and the University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund. Payment is made after approval, within 30 days of the approval letter.

### Option 1 — National Development Fund

A non-refundable contribution of US$230,000 per application. The main applicant may include a spouse, dependent children and dependent parents over 55 with no additional NDF contribution.

### Option 2 — UWI Fund

US$260,000, inclusive of processing fees, for a minimum of six persons; US$10,000 for each additional dependant. One family member receives a one-year, tuition-only scholarship at the University of the West Indies.

## 7. Country at a Glance

### Location

An independent Commonwealth state in the Eastern Caribbean, made up of the islands of Antigua and Barbuda

### Capital

St. John’s

### Area

Antigua 108 sq mi (279.7 km²); Barbuda 62 sq mi (160.6 km²); total 170 sq mi (440.3 km²)

### Population

94,209 (2025 estimate)

### Language

English

### Currency

Eastern Caribbean dollar (XCD), fixed at EC$2.70 to US$1.00

### Time Zone

Atlantic Standard Time (UTC−4), no daylight saving time

### GDP per Capita

US$24,819 (2025, current US$). This is a national average, not a measure of individual income.

### Independence

1981, from the United Kingdom

### Dual Citizenship

Under the Citizenship Act (Cap. 22, ss. 7–11), citizenship is not lost by holding another nationality. Other countries’ rules vary and should be checked.

### Tax

Personal income tax was abolished in 2016. The CIU states there are no capital gains or estate taxes. Other taxes, such as Sales Tax and Property Tax, apply. Take independent tax advice.

### What the Categories Mean

- **Visa-free:** no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- **Visa on arrival:** the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- **eTA:** an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
