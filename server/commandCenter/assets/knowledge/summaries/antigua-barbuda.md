PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Antigua & Barbuda Citizenship by Investment
Citizenship of Antigua & Barbuda can be obtained through a contribution (Option 1 or 2), an approved real estate
investment (Option 3) or an approved business investment (Option 4), with qualifying family members included ina
single application. The programme is administered by the Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU).

OPTIONS 1 AND 2
Contribution Options
Two routes involve a contribution to a government fund: the National Development Fund (NDF) and the University
of the West Indies (UWI) Fund. Payment is made after approval, within 30 days of the approval letter.
OPTION 1 OPTION 2
National Development Fund UWI Fund
A non-refundable contribution of USS230,000 per US$260,000, inclusive of processing fees, for a minimum
application. The main applicant may include a spouse, of six persons; USS10,000 for each additional
dependent children and dependent parents over 55 with dependant. One family member receives a one-year,
no additional NDF contribution. tuition-only scholarship at the University of the West
Indies.
