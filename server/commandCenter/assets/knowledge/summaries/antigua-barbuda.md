## 1. Programme Overview

**Antigua & Barbuda Citizenship by Investment**

Citizenship of Antigua & Barbuda can be obtained through a contribution (Option 1 or 2), an approved real estate investment (Option 3) or an approved business investment (Option 4), with qualifying family members included in a single application. The programme is administered by the Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU).

| Key figure | Detail |
|---|---|
| **9–12 months\*** | Typical time to citizenship, based on current Elevay files |
| **5 days** | Minimum stay required in the five calendar years after citizenship |
| **5 years** | Minimum holding period for the real estate route |
| **144†** | Visa-free, visa-on-arrival and eTA destinations |

\* Based on Elevay's experience processing current files. This is not an official or guaranteed timeline and varies by case.
† Passport Index, accessed 7 October 2026. Indicative only: see Section 8 (Passport Access) for the breakdown, sources and important notice.

Sources: cip.gov.ag, accessed 7 October 2026; cip.gov.ag/citizenship (minimum stay) and cip.gov.ag/investment-options/real-estate (5-year holding period), re-read 1 November 2026. The CIU publishes no processing time; 9–12 months is Elevay's experience.

## 2. Who Can Be Included

- The spouse of the main applicant
- Children aged 0 to 30 who are financially dependent on the main applicant
- Children aged 18 or over who are disabled, live with and are fully supported by the main applicant
- A parent or grandparent of the main applicant or spouse, aged 55 or over and financially dependent on the main applicant
- An unmarried sibling of the main applicant or spouse

### Good to know

- The main applicant must be over 18 years of age.
- Nationals of Afghanistan, Iran, North Korea, Somalia, Sudan and Yemen may apply only if they migrated before the age of majority and/or have held permanent residence elsewhere for at least 10 years with no economic ties to a restricted country.
- Police certificates are required of every applicant over 18, for each country lived in for 6 consecutive months or longer since turning 18.
- Citizenship can be forfeited if the citizen does not spend at least 5 days in Antigua and Barbuda during the five calendar years after obtaining citizenship. Investments are not refunded.

Sources: cip.gov.ag (dependants and eligibility information), accessed 7 October 2026; cip.gov.ag/citizenship (5-day minimum stay and no refund of investment), accessed 1 November 2026.

## 3. Investment Routes

### Investment routes at a glance

| Route | Minimum investment | Holding period |
|---|---|---|
| Option 1: National Development Fund (NDF) | US$230,000 per application | N/A |
| Option 2: University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund | US$260,000 (minimum of six persons per application) | N/A |
| Option 3: Approved real estate | US$300,000 | 5 years |
| Option 4: Approved business investment | US$1,500,000, or US$5,000,000 jointly (each investor at least US$400,000) | 5 years‡ |

Sources: cip.gov.ag, accessed 7 October 2026; Option 3 holding period re-confirmed on cip.gov.ag/investment-options/real-estate, 1 November 2026. The CIU publishes no processing time; 9–12 months is Elevay's experience.
‡ Option 4 holding period: Elevay management confirmation (the CIU business investment page does not state a holding period).

### Option 3: Approved real estate

Property valued at a minimum of US$300,000 in an approved project, supported by a binding purchase agreement. The property cannot be re-sold until 5 years after purchase, unless an alternate officially approved property in Antigua and Barbuda is purchased.

### Option 4: Approved business

An investment of at least US$1,500,000 in an approved business by a single applicant, or a joint investment of at least US$5,000,000 by two or more persons, each contributing at least US$400,000. The investment holding period is 5 years.‡

Government processing fees (Section 5) and due diligence fees (Section 6) apply to Options 3 and 4.

Sources: cip.gov.ag (real estate page, re-read 1 November 2026; business investment page, accessed 7 October 2026); Elevay management confirmation (Option 4 holding period).

## 4. Contribution Options

Two routes involve a contribution to a government fund: the National Development Fund (NDF) and the University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund. Payment is made after approval, within 30 days of the approval letter.

### Option 1: National Development Fund

A non-refundable contribution of **US$230,000 per application**. The main applicant may include a spouse, dependent children and dependent parents over 55 with no additional NDF contribution.

### Option 2: UWI Fund

**US$260,000, inclusive of processing fees, for a minimum of six persons; US$10,000 for each additional dependant.** One family member receives a one-year, tuition-only scholarship at the University of the West Indies.

Sources: cip.gov.ag (NDF and UWI Fund pages), accessed 7 October 2026.

Applies to Option 1 and Options 3 and 4 (US$). For Option 2 (UWI Fund), processing fees are included in the US$260,000.

| Family of five or more | US$20,000 plus US$10,000 for each additional dependant |

|---|---|

| eTA | 6 |
| **Total including eTA** | **144** |

Source: Passport Index (passportindex.org), accessed 7 October 2026. The total is the sum of the three categories shown.

### What the categories mean

- **Visa-free:** no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- **Visa on arrival:** the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- **eTA:** an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
