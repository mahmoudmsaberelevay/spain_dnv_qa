## Program Overview

Spain Non-lucrative Residence

Programme details checked against the Spanish Inclusion Ministry’s Migration information sheets and Royal Decree 1155/2024, accessed 15 October 2026.

Spain’s temporary non-lucrative residence authorisation is for non-EU nationals who have enough of their own means to live in Spain without working. The application is filed in person at the Spanish consulate covering the applicant’s place of residence. It rests on Organic Law 4/2000 and Royal Decree 1155/2024. The route is open.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Means for the main applicant | EUR 28,800 a year, gross. Means for the main applicant: 400% of the IPREM, over 12 months. |
| Initial validity | 1 year from the date of entry into Spain. |
| Decision | Up to 1 month. Maximum from the consulate’s communication. |
| Work | No work. Work or professional activity is not permitted. |

This is a legal maximum, not a promise. At this initial stage, if no decision is issued in that time the request is considered rejected. Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved.

The application is filed in person at the Spanish consulate covering the applicant’s place of residence (or through an external service provider the consulate designates). This route is processed through the consulate, not the UGE.

After the visa is granted, collect the visa within 1 month and enter Spain within its validity (at most 3 months). Within 1 month of entry, apply in person for the foreigner identity card. Foreign documents need a sworn translation and an apostille or legalisation, unless exempt.

Renewal

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Conditions | Means enough to live without working, family included; health insurance kept in force; school-age children enrolled; more than 183 days in Spain in the calendar year; no criminal record. |
| Validity | 2 years, unless a long-term residence permit is more appropriate. |
| When to apply | In the 2 months before expiry. Late filing is possible within 3 months after expiry, although a sanction procedure may be opened. A timely application extends the old authorisation until a decision. |
| Decision | 3 months from filing. If no decision is notified, the renewal may be considered approved by administrative silence. |

Renewal is never promised. Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved.

The holder cannot work in Spain or use this permit to work as a digital nomad. This permit does not allow work or professional activity. It cannot be used as a basis to apply for the digital nomad authorisation, because it does not allow work.

The 183-day condition is an immigration condition. It is not a statement about tax residence. Elevay does not give tax advice. Tax residence and personal tax treatment are not set by the immigration authority. It is very important that you seek your own independent tax advice before you apply.

## Who Can be Included

Who can apply and the means to show

| Condition | What the Ministry says |
|---|---|
| Who | Not an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen or a family member covered by the EU citizen regime; over 18; not in Spain irregularly. |
| Health | Public or private health insurance with an insurer authorised in Spain, plus a medical certificate. No serious disease under the 2005 International Health Regulations. |
| Criminal record | No criminal record in Spain or in the countries lived in during the last 5 years, for offences punishable under Spanish law. |
| Public order | No threat to public order, security or health, and no active commitment not to return to Spain. |
| No work | The authorisation does not allow work or professional activity. If your funds come from a company, the company certifies that you do not work there and you give a sworn statement to the same effect. |
| Main applicant means | 400% of the IPREM. With the IPREM at EUR 600 a month, that is EUR 2,400 a month, or EUR 28,800 a year (12 months, gross). |
| Each dependant means | 100% of the IPREM for each dependant: EUR 7,200 a year (12 months, gross). |

The IPREM is the Spanish public reference income indicator. It is EUR 600 a month under Law 31/2022, the latest figure in the Social Security table at 15 October 2026. The Ministry sheet states the requirement per month; the annual figures are calculated over 12 months. The means must be enough to live without working.

Dependants, such as a spouse or partner and children under 18, can be included in the application. Spouse or registered or proven stable partner and children under 18 can be included in the application (Spanish Foreign Ministry; Article 17 of Organic Law 4/2000).

Children: under 18. Adult children only with a disability that prevents them from supporting themselves (page 5; Article 17 of Organic Law 4/2000, the general family rule).

Each dependant adds 100% of the IPREM to the means required: EUR 7,200 a year, gross (page 3). The means requirement applies for each dependant: 100% of the IPREM each (page 3). Family members appear in the means test and in the school enrolment requirement for minors. Family documents are presented at proposal stage.
