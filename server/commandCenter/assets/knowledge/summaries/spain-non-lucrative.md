PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Spain Non-lucrative Residence
Spain’s temporary non-lucrative residence authorisation is for non-EU nationals who have enough of their own
means to live in Spain without working. The application is filed in person at the Spanish consulate covering the
applicant's place of residence. It rests on Organic Law 4/2000 and Royal Decree 1155/2024. The route is open.

ELIGIBILITY AND MEANS
Who can apply and the means to show
Who Not an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen or a family member covered by the EU citizen regime;
over 18; not in Spain irregularly.
Health Public or private health insurance with an insurer authorised in Spain, plus a medical
certificate. No serious disease under the 2005 International Health Regulations.
Criminal record No criminal record in Spain or in the countries lived in during the last 5 years, for
offences punishable under Spanish law.
Public order No threat to public order, security or health, and no active commitment not to return to
Spain.
No work The authorisation does not allow work or professional activity. If your funds come froma
company, the company certifies that you do not work there and you give a sworn
statement to the same effect.
MEANS TO SHOW
Main applicant 400% of the IPREM. With the IPREM at EUR 600 a month, that is EUR 2,400 a month, or
EUR 28,800 a year (12 months, gross).
Each dependant 100% of the IPREM for each dependant: EUR 7,200 a year (12 months, gross).
The IPREM is the Spanish public reference income indicator. It is EUR 600 a month under Law 31/2022, the latest figure in the Social Security
table at 15 October 2026. The Ministry sheet states the requirement per month; the annual figures are calculated over 12 months. The means
must be enough to live without working.

APPLICATION, VALIDITY AND RENEWAL
How to apply, how long it lasts and how to renew
Where to apply In person at the Spanish consulate covering your place of residence (or through an
external service provider the consulate designates).
Decision A maximum of 1 month from the consulate’s communication. If there is no decision in
that time, the request is considered rejected. This is a legal maximum, not a promise.
Initial validity 1 year from the date of entry into Spain.
After the visa is granted Collect the visa within 1 month and enter Spain within its validity (at most 3 months).
Within 1 month of entry, apply in person for the foreigner identity card.
Documents Foreign documents need a sworn translation and an apostille or legalisation, unless
exempt.
RENEWAL
Conditions Means enough to live without working, family included; health insurance kept in force;
school-age children enrolled; more than 183 days in Spain in the calendar year; no
criminal record.
Validity 2 years, unless a long-term residence permit is more appropriate.
When to apply In the 2 months before expiry. Late filing is possible within 3 months after expiry,
although a sanction procedure may be opened. A timely application extends the old
authorisation until a decision.
Decision 3 months from filing. If no decision is notified, the renewal may be considered approved
by administrative silence.
Renewal is never promised. Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved.
FEES
All fees shall be presented on proposal stage.

FAMILY, WORK AND TAX
Family members, work and tax
FAMILY MEMBERS
Who can be included Dependants, such as a spouse or registered or proven stable partner and children under 18,
can be included in the application (Spanish Foreign Ministry; Article 17 of Organic Law
4/2000).
Children Children under 18. Adult children only with a disability that prevents them from supporting
themselves (Article 17 of Organic Law 4/2000, the general family rule).
Means The means requirement applies for each dependant: 100% of the IPREM each (page 3).
Renewal Family members appear in the means test and in the school enrolment requirement for
minors.
Family documents are presented at proposal stage.
NO WORK IN SPAIN
This permit does not allow work or professional activity. It cannot be used as a basis to apply for the digital nomad
authorisation, because it does not allow work.
TAX: SEEK YOUR OWN ADVICE
Elevay does not give tax advice. Tax residence and personal tax treatment are not set by the immigration authority. It is
very important that you seek your own independent tax advice before you apply.
The 183-day condition on page 4 is an immigration condition. It is not a statement about tax residence.
