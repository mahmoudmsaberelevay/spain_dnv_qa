PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Turkiye Citizenship by Investment
A qualifying investment in Turkiye allows an investor to apply for Turkish citizenship. Five investment routes are
available, and the spouse and children under 18 of the main applicant apply together with the main applicant.
Every application is decided by the President of the Republic of Turkiye, and approval is not guaranteed.
