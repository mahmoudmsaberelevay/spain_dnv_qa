## 1. Program Overview

Türkiye Citizenship by Investment

A qualifying investment in Türkiye allows an investor to apply for Turkish citizenship. Five investment routes are available, and the spouse and children under 18 of the main applicant apply together with the main applicant. Every application is decided by the President of the Republic of Türkiye, and approval is not guaranteed.

## 3. Investment routes

Five investment routes are available.

| Route | Minimum investment | Holding period | Attested by |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Route 1: Real estate | US$400,000 | 3 years (title deed restriction) | Ministry of Environment, Urbanization and Climate Change |
| Route 2: Fixed capital investment | US$500,000 | 3 years‡ | Ministry of Industry and Technology |
| Route 3: Bank deposit | US$500,000 | 3 years | Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency (BRSA) |
| Route 4: Government bonds | US$500,000 | 3 years | Ministry of Treasury and Finance |
| Route 5: Real estate or venture capital fund shares | US$500,000 | 3 years | Capital Markets Board (SPK) |

### Route 1: Real Estate Investment

The investor purchases real estate in Türkiye with a minimum investment of US$400,000. The property cannot be sold for 3 years, and a restriction to this effect is placed on the title deed. The investment is attested by the Ministry of Environment, Urbanization and Climate Change.

### Routes 2 to 5: Capital Investment

Four capital investment routes are available, each with a minimum investment of US$500,000 or equivalent and a minimum holding period of 3 years.

#### Route 2: Fixed capital investment

Minimum investment of US$500,000, attested by the Ministry of Industry and Technology. Holding period: 3 years‡.

#### Route 3: Bank deposit

US$500,000 or equivalent deposited in banks operating in Türkiye, not to be withdrawn for at least 3 years. Attested by the Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency (BRSA).

#### Route 4: Government bonds

US$500,000 or equivalent in government bonds, not to be sold for at least 3 years. Attested by the Ministry of Treasury and Finance.

#### Route 5: Real estate or venture capital fund shares

US$500,000 or equivalent in real estate or venture capital fund shares, not to be sold for at least 3 years. Attested by the Capital Markets Board (SPK).

### How the application works

| Step | What happens |
|---:|---|
| 1 | A certificate of eligibility is obtained from the body that attests the chosen route. |
| 2 | A residence permit is obtained under Law 6458, Article 31(j), at the Provincial Directorate of Immigration Administration. |
| 3 | The citizenship application is made at the Provincial Directorate of Census and Citizenship. |
| 4 | The file is sent to NVIGM for an archive check on national security and public order. |
| 5 | The President decides on the application. Approval is not guaranteed. |

‡The 3-year holding period for the fixed capital route is an Elevay management confirmation; the official sources read do not state a period for this route.

Investment amounts are converted at the Central Bank of Türkiye effective sell or cross rate on the determination date.

Sources: invest.gov.tr, Acquiring property and citizenship (page updated 11 August 2026); the official invest.gov.tr guide (up to date as of 1 January 2024); spk.gov.tr (fund shares route), all accessed 9 October 2026. Processing time, residency requirement, holding period, age limit for children and dual citizenship: Elevay management confirmation, 9 October 2026.

## 8. Passport access

Travel with a Türkiye passport

The number of destinations that a Türkiye passport holder can currently enter without a prior visa application, with a visa on arrival, or with an electronic travel authorisation (eTA).

| Category | Destinations |
|---|---:|
| Visa-free destinations | 71 |
| Visa-on-arrival destinations | 43 |
| eTA destinations | 7 |
| Total including eTA | 121 |

Source: Passport Index (passportindex.org), accessed 4 October 2026. The total is the sum of the three categories shown.

### What the categories mean

- Visa-free: no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- Visa on arrival: the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- eTA: an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
