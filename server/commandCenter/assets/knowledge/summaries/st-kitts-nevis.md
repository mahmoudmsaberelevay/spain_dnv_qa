## 1. Program Overview

Programme details checked against the official St. Kitts & Nevis Citizenship Unit (ciu.gov.kn) in October 2026. Passport access figures from Passport Index as at 4 October 2026.

St. Kitts & Nevis Citizenship by Investment

Citizenship of St. Kitts & Nevis can be obtained through a non-refundable contribution (Option 1 or 2) or an approved real estate investment (Option 3 or 4), with qualifying family members included in a single application. The programme is administered by the St. Kitts & Nevis Citizenship Unit (CIU).

- **4–6 months\*:** Estimated time to citizenship
- **None:** Mandatory residency requirement
- **7 years:** Minimum holding period for real estate routes
- **148†:** Visa-free, visa-on-arrival and eTA destinations

\* Indicative. In certain circumstances processing has been longer due to additional due diligence requirements.

† Passport Index, accessed 4 October 2026. Indicative only: see the passport access page for the breakdown, sources and important notice.

## 2. Who Can be Included

- The spouse of the main applicant
- Children under 18 years of age
- Children aged 18 to 30 in full-time attendance at a recognised secondary or tertiary institution and fully supported by the main applicant
- Children aged 18 or over who are physically or mentally challenged
- Parents of the main applicant or spouse aged 55 or over, living with and fully supported by the main applicant

### GOOD TO KNOW

- The CIU does not accept applications from citizens of Afghanistan, Belarus, Iran, Iraq, North Korea and Russia.
- Biometric enrolment forms part of the application process (required from 14 April 2026).
- A clean criminal record and full due diligence are required of every adult applicant.
- Dual citizenship is permitted: approved applicants are not required to renounce their existing nationality.

### OPTIONS 3 AND 4

### Approved Real Estate Investment

Citizenship can also be obtained by investing in approved real estate. Two real estate routes are available, and both require the property to be held for a minimum of seven years.

### OPTION 3

### Developer real estate

Minimum investment of US$325,000 in an approved development. Resalable under the programme after 7 years.

### OPTION 4

### Private real estate

Minimum investment of US$325,000 for a condominium unit or share in a development, or US$600,000 for a single-family private dwelling home. Held for 7 years.

Sources: ciu.gov.kn (real estate investment pages and application process), accessed 2, 4 and 6 October 2026. Passport fee confirmed by Elevay management, 4 October 2026.

## 4. Contribution options

### OPTIONS 1 AND 2

### Non-Refundable Contribution

Two routes involve a non-refundable contribution and have no holding period: the Sustainable Island State Contribution (SISC) and the Public Benefit Option (PBO). The contribution starts at US$250,000 for the main applicant or for a family of up to four persons (the main applicant plus up to three dependants).

### OPTION 1

### Sustainable Island State Contribution

A non-refundable contribution to the Sustainable Island State Contribution. No holding period.

### OPTION 2

### Public Benefit Option

A non-refundable contribution in a unit of an Approved Public Benefit Project, supporting national infrastructure and development projects. No holding period.

### WHAT THE CATEGORIES MEAN

- Visa-free: no visa needs to be obtained before travel, for the stay permitted by the destination.
- Visa on arrival: the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival.
- eTA: an electronic travel authorisation must be obtained online before departure.
