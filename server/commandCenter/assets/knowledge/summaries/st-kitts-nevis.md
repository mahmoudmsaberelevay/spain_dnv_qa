PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
St. Kitts G Nevis Citizenship by Investment
Citizenship of St. Kitts & Nevis can be obtained through a non-refundable contribution (Option 1 or 2) or an
approved real estate investment (Option 3 or 4), with qualifying family members included ina single application.
The programme is administered by the St. Kitts & Nevis Citizenship Unit (CIU).

OPTIONS 1 AND 2
Non-Refundable Contribution
Two routes involve a non-refundable contribution and have no holding period: the Sustainable Island State
Contribution (SISC) and the Public Benefit Option (PBO). The contribution starts at US$250,000 for the main
applicant or for a family of up to four persons (the main applicant plus up to three dependants).
