## Program Overview

Spain Highly Qualified Professionals

Programme details checked against Law 14/2013 (Spanish Official State Gazette), Orden PJC/44/2026 and the Spanish Inclusion Ministry, accessed 15 October 2026.

Spain’s highly qualified professional route (Article 71 of Law 14/2013) is led by the employer: the company or entity applies for a residence permit for a professional it hires. It covers management and qualified staff of large companies, staff in projects of general interest, and graduates of renowned universities and business schools. The programme is open.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Minimum salary in the contract or job offer (2026) | EUR 41,356.36 a year, gross. |
| Initial authorisation | The length of the employment contract plus 3 months if the contract is shorter than 3 years. It never exceeds 3 years. It allows residence and work throughout Spain (Article 71.3; Sheet 66). |
| Renewal | Renewals run in 2-year periods while the conditions that gave the right still hold (Sheet 67). Renewal is never promised. |
| When to apply | During the 60 calendar days before expiry. A late application is possible within 90 calendar days after expiry, although a sanction procedure may be opened. A timely application extends the old authorisation until a decision. |
| Identity card | After a renewal, request the foreigner identity card in person within one month of notification. |
| Residence authorisation processing time | A maximum of 20 days from electronic filing. If no decision is made in that time, the authorisation is deemed granted by administrative silence (Sheet 66; Article 76.1). |
| Visa from abroad | 10 working days, longer where the Schengen consultation applies (Article 75.5). |

These are legal maximums, not promises, and do not include the time needed to prepare the documents. Nothing in this summary guarantees that an application will be approved. Renewal is never promised: it depends on the conditions still being met. A reduced salary threshold applies in limited cases and is confirmed at proposal stage.

## Who Can be Included

Who can apply and the salary to show

| Condition | What the law and the Ministry say |
|---|---|
| Category (a) | Management or highly qualified staff of large companies or groups. |
| Category (b) | Staff in projects of general interest, certified by the Directorate-General for International Trade and Investments. |
| Category (c) | Graduates and postgraduates of renowned universities and business schools. |
| Employer application | The employer applies; the professional cannot apply alone. |
| Company tests (category a) | The employer meets one of: more than 250 workers in Spain; annual net turnover over EUR 50 million (or own funds over EUR 43 million); average gross inward investment of at least EUR 1 million over the previous 3 years; or an investment stock over EUR 3 million. Small and medium businesses in a strategic sector, certified by the Directorate-General, can also qualify. |
| Company registration | The company’s registration with the UGE is valid for 3 years and renewable. Any change must be notified within 30 days. |
| Qualification | National route: a degree comparable to Level 1 of the Spanish higher education framework, or 3 years of equivalent professional experience. EU Blue Card route: a Level 2 qualification (at least 3 years of higher education) or 5 years of comparable experience, plus a job contract or firm offer of at least 6 months. |
| Minimum salary | The contract or firm job offer must state a gross annual salary of at least EUR 41,356.36. This is 1.4 times the average annual gross salary per worker published by the Spanish statistics institute (Orden PJC/44/2026). |
| Salary updates | The amount is updated by the Ministry when a new salary survey is published, and applies to applications filed from one month after publication. A reduced threshold applies in limited cases; it is confirmed at proposal stage. The figure is correct at 15 October 2026. |
| General conditions | Over 18, no criminal record, health insurance, sufficient resources and the fee paid (Article 62.3). |

Spouse or partner

Spouse or person in an analogous affective relationship. An unmarried, unregistered partner shows at least 1 year of continuous cohabitation, unless there are common children.

Children

Minor children, and adult children who depend economically on the holder and have not formed their own family unit. Aged 18 to under 26: no income from work or public benefits, and enrolled in a qualifying course or registered as a job-seeker. Aged 26 or over: only with an incapacity that prevents them from being economically independent.

Parents and grandparents

Dependent ascendants show economic or physical dependence; over 80 is presumed. Family members may apply with the holder or later.

Family application: filed by the family member, the holder or the hiring entity; the same fee model applies to holder and dependants. These age and dependence rules come from the UGE family guidance. {FAMDOC}

Family members apply with the Ministry’s family application form and must show sufficient economic resources. The amount is confirmed at proposal stage.

Tax: seek your own advice

Elevay does not give tax advice. Tax residence and personal tax treatment are not set by the immigration authority. It is very important that you seek your own independent tax advice before you apply.
