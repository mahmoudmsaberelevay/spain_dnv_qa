PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
Spain Highly Qualified Professionals
Spain's highly qualified professional route (Article 71 of Law 14/2013) is led by the employer: the company or
entity applies for a residence permit for a professional it hires. lt covers management and qualified staff of large
companies, staff in projects of general interest, and graduates of renowned universities and business schools. The
programme is open.

ELIGIBILITY AND SALARY
Who can apply and the salary to show
Category (a) Management or highly qualified staff of large companies or groups.
Category (b) Staff in projects of general interest, certified by the Directorate-General for
International Trade and Investments.
Category (c) Graduates and postgraduates of renowned universities and business schools.
Company tests (category The employer meets one of: more than 250 workers in Spain; annual net turnover over
a) EUR 50 million (or own funds over EUR 43 million); average gross inward investment of at
least EUR 1 million over the previous 3 years; or an investment stock over EUR 3 million.
Small and medium businesses ina strategic sector, certified by the Directorate-General,
can also qualify.
Company registration The company’s registration with the UGE is valid for 3 years and renewable. Any change
must be notified within 30 days.
Qualification National route: a degree comparable to Level 1 of the Spanish higher education
framework, or 3 years of equivalent professional experience. EU Blue Card route: a Level
2 qualification (at least 3 years of higher education) or 5 years of comparable
experience, plus a job contract or firm offer of at least 6 months.
General conditions Over 18, no criminal record, health insurance, sufficient resources and the fee paid
(Article 62.3).
SALARY TO SHOW
Minimum salary The contract or firm job offer must state a gross annual salary of at least EUR
41,356.36. This is 1.4 times the average annual gross salary per worker published by the
Spanish statistics institute (Orden PJC/44/2026).
The amount is updated by the Ministry when a new salary survey is published, and applies to applications filed from one month after
publication. A reduced threshold applies in limited cases; it is confirmed at proposal stage. The figure is correct at 15 October 2026. “UGE” is
the Large Companies and Strategic Groups Unit of the Spanish Inclusion Ministry (Unidad de Grandes Empresas y Colectivos Estratégicos).

VALIDITY, RENEWAL AND TIMING
How long it lasts, how to renew and how long it can
take
Initial authorisation The length of the employment contract plus 3 months if the contract is shorter than 3
years. It never exceeds 3 years. It allows residence and work throughout Spain (Article
71.3; Sheet 66).
Renewal Renewals run in 2-year periods while the conditions that gave the right still hold (Sheet
67). Renewal is never promised.
When to apply During the 60 calendar days before expiry. A late application is possible within 90
calendar days after expiry, although a sanction procedure may be opened. A timely
application extends the old authorisation until a decision.
Identity card After a renewal, request the foreigner identity card in person within one month of
notification.
PROCESSING TIME (LEGAL MAXIMUM)
Residence authorisation A maximum of 20 days from electronic filing. If no decision is made in that time, the
authorisation is deemed granted by administrative silence (Sheet 66; Article 76.1).
Visa from abroad 10 working days, longer where the Schengen consultation applies (Article 75.5).
TIMING
These are legal maximums, not promises, and do not include the time needed to prepare the documents. Nothing in this
summary guarantees that an application will be approved.
FEES
All fees shall be presented on proposal stage.

FAMILY AND TAX
Family members and tax
FAMILY MEMBERS
Spouse or partner Spouse or person in an analogous affective relationship. An unmarried, unregistered
partner shows at least 1 year of continuous cohabitation, unless there are common children.
Children Minor children, and adult children who depend economically on the holder and have not
formed their own family unit. Aged 18 to under 26: no income from work or public benefits,
and enrolled in a qualifying course or registered as a job-seeker. Aged 26 or over: only with
an incapacity that prevents them from being economically independent.
Parents and Dependent ascendants show economic or physical dependence; over 80 is presumed. Family
grandparents members may apply with the holder or later.
Family application: filed by the family member, the holder or the hiring entity; the same fee model applies to holder and dependants. These age
and dependence rules come from the UGE family guidance. {FAMDOC}
FUNDS FOR THE FAMILY
Family members apply with the Ministry's family application form and must show sufficient economic resources. The
amount is confirmed at proposal stage.
The law applies without prejudice to the applicant's tax and Social Security obligations (Article 62.5).
TAX: SEEK YOUR OWN ADVICE
Elevay does not give tax advice. Tax residence and personal tax treatment are not set by the immigration authority. It is
very important that you seek your own independent tax advice before you apply.
