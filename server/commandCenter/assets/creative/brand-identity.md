# ELEVAY Brand Operating System (Master Enterprise Edition, source of truth)

This is the authoritative, complete ELEVAY brand identity. It supersedes any other/older ELEVAY brand data. Always apply these rules when producing ELEVAY marketing content — captions, static post copy/design specs, reel/video scripts and prompts, campaign plans, landing page copy, email, or AI image/video generation prompts.

## 1. Company & Positioning

- **Company:** ELEVAY — Global Mobility Consultancy (Residency & Citizenship by Investment, Digital Nomad Visas, Business Immigration, International Relocation, non-legal tax/investment planning guidance, family future planning, international business expansion).
- **Primary markets:** Middle East, North Africa, GCC.
- **Target audience:** High-net-worth individuals, entrepreneurs/business owners, families seeking global mobility, remote workers/digital nomads, investors, professionals relocating internationally.
- **Positioning:** A Strategic Global Mobility Advisory Firm, positioned alongside premium consulting firms (McKinsey/Deloitte/Stripe-tier feel). **NOT** an immigration broker, travel agency, "just a visa consultancy," or quick-fix provider.
- **Mission:** Helping individuals and families secure a better future through strategic residency and citizenship solutions.
- **Vision:** To become the Middle East's most trusted international mobility consultancy.
- **Brand promise:** Helping people build secure futures through ethical guidance, strategic planning, and premium client service.
- **Personality:** Professional, premium, trustworthy, international, sophisticated, educational, modern, reliable, honest, knowledgeable, supportive, strategic, visionary, family-oriented, investment-oriented, future-planner, solution-focused. **Never:** arrogant, salesy, flashy, aggressive.
- **Archetypes:** The Sage (primary — expertise/analysis), The Caregiver (secondary — family security/support), The Ruler (supporting — authority/professionalism in visuals).
- **Core values:** Integrity, transparency, long-term relationships, knowledge, innovation, global perspective, trust, security, family first, professional excellence, continuous improvement, confidentiality, ethical advisory.

## 2. Logo Rules (ABSOLUTE)

- Official logo file: `elevay_logo_official.png`, transparent PNG, 402×446px, 1:1 aspect ratio. Colors: Baby Blue (#5BA3B8) + Dark Navy (#3D4750).
- Never modify colors, shape, or design. Never stretch/compress/rotate. Never add shadows/glows/gradients. Never crop or remove parts. Never redesign or simplify. Always keep 1:1 ratio and minimum 10px clear space.
- Sizing: Web 80–500px (profile 200px), Social 100–400px, Business card 0.5–2in @300DPI, Letterhead 1–3in @300DPI, Video/reel outro 200–400px centered on white, Favicon 16–64px, Signage 4–24in.
- Approved backgrounds: Pure White #FFFFFF (best), Off-White #FFEBDA, Cream #FFE7D1, transparent, Light Teal #B3CFD4 (acceptable), Dark Navy #3D4750 (use white/light logo version). Avoid busy patterns, low contrast, unfiltered photos, unapproved gradients, non-brand colors.
- Reel/video outro: static logo, 3 seconds, centered on pure white background, no animation.

## 3. Color System

**Cool palette (structure/text, 40–50% of design):**
- Dark Navy `#3D4750` — primary text, headings
- Navy Blue `#445563` — secondary text, body copy
- Medium Navy `#4B6475` — borders, dividers
- Baby Blue `#5BA3B8` — primary accent, logo
- Light Blue `#6475FF` — links, interactive
- Bright Blue `#4750FF` — hover/emphasis

**Warm palette (accents/CTAs, 15–20%):**
- Pale Gold `#FFEED3`, Soft Gold `#EED38E`, Warm Gold `#EACA75`, Bright Gold `#FFBF5D` (CTA buttons), Deep Gold `#C4985D`, Warm Coral `#FF9D4A`

**Neutral palette (backgrounds/space, 30–40%):**
- Off-White `#FFEBDA` — primary background
- Cream `#FFE7D1` — card backgrounds
- Light Teal `#B3CFD4` — subtle accents/borders
- Soft Gray `#CCDBD5` — dividers
- Pure White `#FFFFFF` — text on dark backgrounds
- Dark Gray `#4D5964` — alternative text

**Critical rule: 90% of colors in any design must come from this palette.** Only 10% flexibility for photography/gradients/effects.

Key accessible pairings: Dark Navy on Off-White (12.5:1, AAA), Dark Navy on Pure White (13.8:1, AAA), Bright Gold on Dark Navy (7.1:1, AAA button text), Baby Blue on Off-White (5.2:1, AA).

Gradients: Primary = Dark Navy → Baby Blue. Warm = Deep Gold → Bright Gold. Neutral = Off-White → Cream.

## 4. Typography

- **Font family:** Apex Sans — Book ST (body/regular), Medium ST (headings/buttons/emphasis), Medium Italic ST (quotes/emphasis only, never long body text), Medium C (condensed — nav/labels/tags only, never body or headings).
- Headings: Apex Sans Medium ST/C, color Dark Navy or Baby Blue, max 2 heading levels per section.
- Body: Apex Sans Book ST, Dark Navy on light bg / Pure White on dark bg, min 16px desktop, 1.6 line height.
- Desktop sizes: H1 48px, H2 36px, H3 28px, H4 22px, Body 16px, Small 14px.
- Mobile sizes: H1 32px, H2 28px, H3 22px, Body 14px.

## 5. Photography & Visual Direction

- Style: high-quality luxury lifestyle photography, real/authentic moments, professional people, premium settings. Natural lighting, rule of thirds, shallow depth of field (f/2.8–f/4.0), eye-level or slightly elevated angles, warm inviting color grading matching the palette. Mood: aspirational, optimistic, trustworthy, premium.
- Approved themes: families (multi-gen, elegant homes, travel), professionals (executives, offices, consultations), European architecture (landmarks, premium residences), business meetings, luxury lifestyle/travel, investment/wealth planning, global mobility/international cities, education, children (educational/family bonding), iconic international city skylines.
- **Never use:** fake/artificial-posed stock photos, artificial handshakes, passport stamp imagery, overused flag imagery, low-resolution/cheap imagery, overly staged scenes, clichéd business photography.
- **People shown must appear Arab/Middle Eastern in modern elegant clothing** — do not depict American, African, Asian, or European-looking people as the subjects. Never show passport images.
- Iconography: outline style, 1.5–2px stroke, 2–3px corner radius, 24×24px grid, monochrome Dark Navy or Baby Blue only.
- Cards: Cream or Pure White background, 1px Light Teal border, soft shadow `0px 4px 12px rgba(61,71,80,0.1)`, 8–12px radius.

## 6. Social Media & Content Rules

- **Captions:** written in Modern Standard Arabic (unless a specific language is requested).
- **On-image/on-video design text:** English only.
- **Never use "Visa" (تأشيرة)** — always "Residency" (إقامة) or "Residency." Use "Investment" (استثمار), "Citizenship" (المواطنة) appropriately.
- Caption structure: **Hook** (5–10 words, strong opening) → **Body** (2–3 sentences on benefits) → **CTA** (contact/learn more). Target length 100–150 words.
- Never embed phone numbers, emails, WhatsApp numbers, QR codes, or URLs inside images or videos (fine in caption text/bio only, per general rule — but avoid putting them in the caption creative body per compliance section too; keep contact info to bio/link-in-bio, not hardcoded into the caption unless explicitly requested).
- Tone: professional, premium, trustworthy, luxury, educational, persuasive without exaggeration.

### Static posts (Instagram/Facebook)
- Format: 1:1 (1080×1080) for feed, 4:5 for reach.
- No rectangular photo frames — editorial diagonal/asymmetric layouts. No circles/squares/basic geometric shapes as design elements.
- Logo always present, fully transparent background.
- English text inside the design; Arabic in the caption only.

### Reels & video
- Format: 9:16 vertical, minimum 720×1280 (spec also says 1080p min quality), 15–60 seconds.
- **No text overlays inside the video** — background music only, unless voice-over is specifically requested.
- End every reel with the ELEVAY logo centered on a clean white/pure-white background, static, 3 seconds, no animation.
- People on screen: Arab/Middle Eastern appearance, modern elegant clothing. Staff appearances use official staff photos/AI avatars only. Never show passport images.
- Avoid: stock footage, artificial scenes, passport imagery, flag imagery, text overlays.

### Carousels
- Format 4:5 or 1:1, consistent visual style across slides, first slide is a strong hook visual.

3. Long-term Planning — build sustainable, legal pathways to global citizenship
4. Premium Service — expert guidance from trusted advisors

**Avoid these terms:** Visa (تأشيرة), Broker, Immigration Agent, Quick Fix, Guaranteed, Easy, Instant, Shortcut.

**Copy specs:**
- Landing page: headline 8–12 words (benefit-focused), subheading, 3–4 body paragraphs (what/who/benefits/next steps), clear CTA, 300–400 words total.
- Social caption: Arabic, hook-body-CTA, 100–150 words, no phone/URLs/QR in the caption.
- Email: professional/personalized/consultative tone, greeting → value prop → benefits → CTA → closing, 150–250 words.
- Presentation decks: one clear benefit-focused headline per slide, 3–5 key points per slide, premium imagery, ELEVAY palette, clear CTA/contact.

## 8. Compliance (mandatory, non-negotiable)

**Always include appropriate disclaimers where relevant:**
- "Subject to government approval"
- "Based on individual circumstances"
- "Consult with legal advisors"
- "Terms and conditions apply"

**Never:**
- Guarantee approval of applications, processing timelines, citizenship outcomes, or success rates
- Guarantee visa/residency issuance
- Use fake urgency ("Only 3 spots left!") or false scarcity
- Use deceptive claims ("Guaranteed approval") or misleading statistics
- Use unverified testimonials
- Provide specific legal or tax advice — always point to professional consultation
- Misrepresent government programs; verify country-specific legal requirements before publishing
