## 1. Colors to Use

Use approximately **90% ELEVAY brand colors** in every designed asset. Photography may introduce natural environmental colors, but the designed elements, typography, panels, accents, borders, CTA elements, and logos must remain within the ELEVAY palette.

### Primary colors

| Color | HEX | Main use |
|---|---|---|
| **Off-White** | `#FFEBDA` | Main background, editorial text panels, warm negative space |
| **Cream** | `#FFE7D1` | Secondary backgrounds, cards, soft warm surfaces |
| **Dark Navy** | `#3D4750` | Headlines, primary text, structured elements |
| **Navy Blue** | `#445563` | Secondary text and supporting information |
| **Medium Navy** | `#4B6475` | Dividers, borders, subtle structural details |
| **Baby Blue / ELEVAY Blue** | `#5BA3B8` | Logo bird, accents, location details, icons, highlights |
| **Light Teal** | `#B3CFD4` | Thin borders, subtle dividers, soft background accents |
| **Soft Gray** | `#CCDBD5` | Secondary separators and quiet neutral details |
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | Video outro background, clean space, light-logo applications |

### Gold accents

| Color | HEX | Main use |
|---|---|---|
| **Bright Gold** | `#FFBF5D` | Small CTA buttons, tags, restrained highlights |
| **Soft Gold** | `#EED38E` | Secondary highlights and gentle accents |
| **Warm Gold** | `#EACA75` | Small decorative details |
| **Deep Gold** | `#C4985D` | Fine borders or text-overlay accents when appropriate |
| **Social Gold Border** | `#EBD990` | Optional thin social-media border |

### Dark-background option

Use **Deep Navy `#0A1628`** only when a dark-background creative is specifically required. On a dark background, use the approved light/white logo version with sufficient contrast. Do not place the standard dark wordmark on a dark background if it becomes difficult to read.

### Color ratio

- **Cool navy and blue:** structure, typography, identity
- **Warm off-white and cream:** breathing room and premium softness
- **Gold:** small accents only, never the dominant color
- **Photography:** warm, natural, realistic, and editorial
- Keep designed color usage restrained and balanced; do not make the creative visually loud.

## 2. Colors and Visual Treatments Not to Use

Do not use the following unless Mahmoud explicitly requests a special campaign exception:

- Neon colors or fluorescent colors
- Bright electric purple, magenta, lime green, cyan, or unrelated vivid blue
- Harsh pure black as a dominant background or heavy shadow color
- Random red, orange, green, or purple brand accents
- Unapproved gradients, rainbow gradients, or glowing color effects
- Large gold backgrounds or oversized gold visual areas
- Low-contrast text that is difficult to read
- Clashing colors outside the ELEVAY palette
- Artificial color grading, excessive saturation, fake HDR, or plastic-looking skin
- Heavy grain, distressed textures, loud patterns, or busy decorative backgrounds
- Harsh black drop shadows
- Excessive glassmorphism or decorative blur
- Cheap Canva-style templates, cluttered collages, or dense infographic layouts
- Sharp corners; use restrained rounded corners where a designed card or CTA needs them
- Excessive rounding; standard corners should generally be 8–16px, with pills reserved for tags and CTAs

### Mandatory visual restrictions

- No passport images
- No flags as decorative hero objects
- No approval seals, government-looking stamps, or false official marks
- No portals, sculptures, museum installations, abstract immigration symbols, or geological cross-sections
- No QR codes, phone numbers, email addresses, WhatsApp numbers, or URLs inside images/videos unless specifically requested
- No guarantee, approval, or outcome claims
- No clickbait or “act now” pressure

## 3. Logo Placement and Logo Rules

### Official logo rule

Always use the **exact official ELEVAY logo**. The logo includes:

- Origami Arctic Tern bird
- ELEVAY wordmark
- `EXPANDING YOUR FREEDOM` tagline where the full logo is required

Never redraw, simplify, recolor, stretch, compress, rotate, crop, distort, animate, or AI-generate the logo.

### Logo colors

- Bird: official Baby Blue `#5BA3B8`
- Wordmark: official Dark Navy `#3D4750`
- Use the official white/light version only on an approved dark background.

### Clear space and sizing

- Maintain at least **10px clear space** on every side for digital layouts.
- Keep the logo at a readable, proportional size.
- Typical digital/social width: **100–400px**, depending on canvas size.
- Video/reel outro width: normally **200–400px**, centered.
- Never allow the logo to touch a text block, edge, border, CTA, or important photographic subject.

### Placement by content type

| Asset | Placement rule |
|---|---|
| **Static post** | Use a quiet corner with sufficient clear space, usually top-left, bottom-right, or another intentional editorial position that does not interfere with the message. |
| **Carousel** | Use the first or final slide as the primary branded slide; keep placement consistent across the set. |
| **PDF/program summary** | Use the approved header position, normally top-left, with the exact wordmark and tagline when the template requires the full logo. |
| **Video/reel narrative scenes** | Do not add a floating or AI-generated logo over narrative footage unless explicitly approved. Keep narrative frames clean. |
| **Video/reel outro** | Use the exact official logo centered on a pure white background. This is the primary video-branding location. |

### Logo prohibitions

- No logo on passports, fake documents, government papers, legal pages, or misleading official objects.
- No logo printed onto a notebook, laptop, wall, sign, clothing item, or prop unless explicitly approved for that specific creative.
- No logo watermark over faces or important subjects.
- No logo with glow, shadow, 3D extrusion, gradient, outline, or metallic effect.

## 6. People, Wardrobe, and Realism Rules

Every person must be Arab/Middle Eastern and presented in modern, elegant clothing appropriate to their age, role, gender presentation, environment, and occasion.

### Mandatory wardrobe rule

Every person must have a complete, coherent, realistic outfit from head to toe. Formal or business suits must always use appropriate polished formal footwear such as dress shoes or elegant loafers.

Never pair a suit with:

- Slippers
- Flip-flops
- Beach sandals
- House shoes
- Casual footwear
- Visibly inappropriate sneakers

Do not add turbans, keffiyeh, ghutra, shemagh, scarves, or random traditional accessories unless Mahmoud explicitly requests them. Do not mix traditional clothing with modern Western businesswear without explicit direction.

### Frame-by-frame QC

Before final delivery, inspect every person in every relevant shot for:

- Complete outfit and footwear
- Correct garment fit
- Stable accessories
- Natural anatomy and hands
- No extra fingers or duplicated items
- Grounded feet and believable shadows
- No floating garments
- No footwear pop-in or disappearance
- No wardrobe mutations between shots
- Coherent movement, lighting, and continuity

The final result must feel physically filmed, not like a slideshow or animated still image.

## 7. Photography and Creative Direction

Use warm, natural-light, premium editorial photography. The visual should feel like a luxury architecture, real-estate, or international lifestyle campaign.

Preferred subjects include:

- Authentic European architecture
- Elegant apartments, terraces, offices, cafés, coastlines, and city streets
- Arab/Middle Eastern professionals and families in contemporary clothing
- Natural remote-work, advisory, family, and lifestyle moments
- Quiet confidence, freedom, stability, and future planning

Avoid:

- Generic stock travel photography
- Passport or visa imagery
- Unrealistic portals or fantasy immigration symbols
- Overly posed business handshakes
- Impossible camera movement
- Unreachable objects and physically inconsistent scenes
- Distorted architecture or duplicated people
- Random cultural accessories
- AI-generated brand marks

## 8. Typography and Copy Rules

### Typography

- Primary font: **Apex Sans**
- Substitute: **Plus Jakarta Sans**
- Fallback: system sans-serif such as `-apple-system`, `Segoe UI`, or another clean geometric sans-serif
- Headings: Medium, approximately weight 500
- Body copy: Book/Regular, approximately weight 400
- Use generous line height, normally around 1.55 for body copy.

### Casing

Use sentence case:

- Correct: `Book a consultation`
- Incorrect: `BOOK A CONSULTATION`
- Incorrect: `Book A Consultation`

All caps are reserved for acronyms and approved logo/tagline treatments.

### Messaging

ELEVAY copy must be:

- Factual
- Educational
- Reassuring
- Family-oriented when relevant
- Future-focused
- Transparent and advisory
- Clear without unnecessary jargon

Do not use:

- Approval guarantees
- Immigration guarantees
- Investment-return guarantees
- False scarcity
- “Act now!” urgency
- Exaggerated superlatives without support
- Unverified numbers
