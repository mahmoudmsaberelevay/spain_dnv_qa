## 9. Final Pre-Delivery Checklist

### Color

- [ ] At least 90% of designed colors come from the ELEVAY palette.
- [ ] Gold is restrained and used only as an accent.
- [ ] Text contrast is clear and readable.
- [ ] No unapproved neon, unrelated colors, harsh black shadows, or fake HDR.

### Logo

- [ ] Exact official logo source used.
- [ ] Bird, wordmark, and tagline preserved where the full logo is required.
- [ ] No stretching, cropping, recoloring, rotation, glow, shadow, or redesign.
- [ ] At least 10px clear space maintained.
- [ ] Video logo appears centered on the clean white outro.

### Video

- [ ] Vertical 9:16 format for reels.
- [ ] No narrative on-screen text unless explicitly requested.
- [ ] Outro holds the exact logo statically for 3 seconds.
- [ ] Transitions are smooth and restrained.
- [ ] Music, ambience, and narration are balanced.

### Voice-over

- [ ] Egyptian Arabic by default, unless English is explicitly requested.
- [ ] Scene-based, concise, natural script.
- [ ] Thoughtful, warm, premium delivery.
- [ ] No guarantees or exaggerated claims.
- [ ] Narration is clear above the music.
- [ ] The logo outro is normally free of narration.

### Realism

- [ ] Every person is Arab/Middle Eastern and appropriately styled.
- [ ] Every person has complete head-to-toe wardrobe continuity.
- [ ] Formal clothing has matching polished formal footwear.
- [ ] No forbidden headwear or random accessories.
- [ ] No anatomy, hand, shadow, object, movement, or spatial-logic errors.

## Master brand additions

- [ ] Positioning is strategic global mobility advisory, with audience and messaging pillar appropriate to the brief.
- [ ] Social captions use Modern Standard Arabic by default; designed text is English unless the user explicitly overrides it.
- [ ] Video contains no text overlays or narration unless requested.
- [ ] Master palette, format, typography, icon, and card rules are applied where relevant.
- [ ] Static layouts avoid rectangular photo frames and decorative basic geometric shapes.
- [ ] Native logo proportions are preserved; a square canvas uses padding, not distortion.
- [ ] The default outro ends with the static logo on white.
- [ ] Marketing terminology is on-brand without misrepresenting official program categories.
- [ ] Current program claims are verified; source figures and contrast labels are not assumed accurate.
- [ ] Relevant disclaimers are placed appropriately; no guarantees or unverified testimonials appear.
- [ ] Contact details are omitted from creative/caption bodies unless explicitly requested.
